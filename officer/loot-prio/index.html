Player > class trumps every decision on this sheet — the list is where the call starts, not where it ends.

It cannot see who carried the night, who swapped spec to save a pull, or who has waited three weeks without a drop. Council decides; this only makes it faster.

Only items two or more classes compete for are listed. If everyone who can use a drop is the same class they roll it out between themselves, and an item nobody in the raid can use needs no page — both are left off so this stays short enough to read mid-raid.
