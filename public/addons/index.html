Addons & WeakAuras

Every download is the latest version straight from the source. A gold NEW badge means there's an update you haven't downloaded yet — it clears when you hit Download.

  1. Downloadthe .zip of each addon below
  2. Extractthe folder into Interface\AddOns\ in your WoW folder
  3. /reloadin game, and you are set

Folder ends in -main or -master? Rename it to drop that suffix, or the game will not load it.
