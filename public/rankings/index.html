RATS — Rankings & Hall of Fame
ℹ️ How these rankings work — (click)

Ranked by a blend of 50% raw output (total damage/healing — rewards showing up to every raid) and 50% server percentile — how you rank against others of your own class/spec on the server (Warrior vs Warrior, not vs everyone). Fairer across classes: a Warrior in the top 2% of Warriors can outrank a class with a higher raw ceiling.

The big DPS/HPS number is a bayesian average — a couple of fights get pulled toward the guild mean, so a lucky 1–2 fight spike can't top the board. More fights = your real average shows.

Only guild members count (pugs excluded). Alts fold into the main, but a person's DPS toon and healer toon stay in their own tables. Tank fights (Protection) don't count toward DPS, and your roster main spec decides which table you're in.

  • 📈 Most improved — biggest % jump vs the previous raid.
  • 📉 Needs work — below the guild average for their role.
  • 🏅 Records — biggest single parses ever (a short fight like Hodir inflates DPS — that's why the boss is shown).

Filters (Week / Month / All, per raid and 25/10-man) are all client-side — no reload. Tanking is coming once the log API exposes damage-taken.
