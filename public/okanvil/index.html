PuG builder /okanvil → PuG

Tired of building your PuG by hand?

Spamming trade, counting heals in your head, retyping the LFM every time someone joins, scrolling whispers to find who said "rsham 5.8k"… Okanvil does the bookkeeping.

[4. LookingForGroup] [Okanor]: LFM ICC25 need 2 Tank 4 Heal 8 Melee 10 Ranged 4.8+ gs (B+O+P res)
  • Tank / Healer / Melee / Ranged slots that fill themselves as people whisper
  • The LFM line rebuilds as you pick — raid, size, min GS, reserves
  • Reserve chips: BoE, Orbs, Patterns, Fragments, Shards, Mount, Key
  • Reads specs and gearscore off whoever applies
Raid Finder

Looking for a raid, not a wall of trade spam?

Every LFM in chat becomes one clean row: raid, GS, the roles they still need, whether loot is reserved and what is reserved — and whether you're already saved there.

  • Decodes raid lingo: B+O+P, HR, DBW, DC, "3Heals 7Dps"
  • Hard-reserved items shown as real item links
  • One click to whisper or join; sort by raid, GS, leader or age
Loot Council

A loot council people actually trust.

The master looter picks the items, every raider answers BIS · Upgrade · Small · OS · Pass in a popup, and the council sees each answer next to what that raider is wearing — ilvl gap included.

  • Every officer present gets the board and one vote per item
  • The guild's priority ladder right on the board
  • The ML still decides — votes are advice, not autopilot
  • Test mode with your own gear, nothing ever handed out
Loot history · Mini Roll

"Who won that ring on Anub'arak?"

Every drop, every boss, every night — who got it and how: MS roll 87, council · BIS, or disenchanted. The Mini Roll window runs the roll-off and records the winner for you.

[Raid Warning] [Okanor]: Roll [Acidmaw Treads] -- MAIN SPEC /roll (1-100)
  • Sessions by raid, boss cards, heroic / 25 badges
  • Speed-run auto master-loot to your collectors
  • One-click export to the RATS hub
Snapshots

Forgot who was on last week's run?

At the first pull Okanvil takes a snapshot of the raid: every group, every player, their real spec — scanned, not guessed — and who was main tank. Attendance without anyone keeping a spreadsheet.

  • Automatic — nothing to remember to press
  • Invite the whole roster back for the next run
  • Export attendance straight to the hub
Raid notes

Your raid plan, on a timer, in everyone's face.

MRT-format notes per boss, size and difficulty. Timed lines count down in the fight and light up for the person they name — no more "who's on infest 3?".

  • Follows the room: walk in, the right note opens
  • Send to raid, see who has it
  • Learns the boss's real spell ids from your own pulls
Recruit

Recruiting while you're busy raiding.

Your advert goes out on its own. Whisper inv and you're guild-invited; ask anything else and you get the Discord link — even when you're AFK. Every conversation lands in an Inbox, so nobody who answered gets lost.

  • Keyword auto-invite and auto-replies
  • Inbox with unread count and one-click whisper back
  • Officers share one advert across the guild

Install in a minute.

Works on the normal and HD 3.3.5a clients. Turn off any module you don't want in Modules — the rest stays out of your way.

Download latest
  1. Download the zipAlways the latest release, straight from GitHub.
  2. Extract it into AddOnsWorld of Warcraft\Interface\AddOns\Okanvil — if the folder ends in -main, rename it.
  3. Start the gamePick your modules in the welcome window, then /okanvil any time.
