# 🧪 The Sewer Healer

The Blood Queen has a cruel sense of humour. She likes to take the Light first.

She marked **<@146677218280603648>** with her shadows, and the fire began to crawl across the stone behind him. A paladin knows what to do with fire. You walk out of it.

Then she screamed.

Terror. Every rat in the hall lost his legs at once, and the Lightkeeper ran blind, straight back through his own shadow fire. When the fear broke, his mace and his shield were lying on the floor, and the amber runes on his plate had gone dark.

The raid had one healer left. Then the Queen rose into the air.

**<@492483081849012234>**, the ClawFather, stood under the bolts with his staff raised and his starlight burning. The air phase took him anyway. The starfall went out over the hall, and his spirit-cat faded into the dark.

Then there was one.

**<@253555199199674368>**. No armour. A rag around the waist. Eyes too wide. A jar of green sewer brew in one hand that no sane healer would carry into a raid.

He did not panic. He laughed.

The jar came up. The green light poured down over the raid, and the storm answered him from above. Tank, mages, warriors, every rat in the hall drinking from the sewer whether he liked it or not. One healer, one jar, nine bars of health, and a Queen who wanted all of them.

She came down from the air hungry. She found every rat still standing.

And the Blood Queen of Icecrown fell to a raid kept alive by sewer water.

Okanor watched it from the floor. Every rat in the guild owes that shaman a wheel of cheese.

*The Light fell first. The sewer held.*

*So it is written.*
