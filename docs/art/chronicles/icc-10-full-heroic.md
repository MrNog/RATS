## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

Ready to post.

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# 💀 THE HEROIC TEN

They said it on the realm. In trade chat. Some of them even said it in the Sewer.

*"Full heroic? Those rats? Never."*

Heroic is not the citadel you know. Every boss hits harder, every mistake costs the whole raid, and there is no room for panic, no room for a hero charge, no room for ten rats doing ten different things.

So the Rats did the one thing nobody expected from a sewer swarm.

**They listened.**

One voice made the calls, and ten rats moved on it. Spread when it said spread. Stack when it said stack. Stop when it said stop. When a pull went wrong, nobody screamed. Somebody breathed, somebody said *"again"*, and the swarm ran back in, calm.

The Spire fell. The Plagueworks fell. The Crimson Hall and the Frostwing Halls fell. Eleven bosses on heroic, one after another.

Then the top of the world, and the king in the frozen crown, at his full strength this time.

He did not get a long night. He got **nuked.** ❄️💥

Frostmourne broke. The Lich King fell. And the throne that heroic guilds fight for over many weeks answered, once again, to the Rats.

To everyone who doubted us: thank you. You made the cheese taste better. 🧀

We are not the loudest guild on the realm. We are the one that listens, keeps calm, and does not stop. That is what strong looks like.
```
