## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

Written with paragraph **D**, a few good tries, which is how the night went.

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# 👑 The Swarm Takes the Throne

Once, ten rats climbed the Frozen Throne and came back down as Kingslayers. The Sewer was proud. The Sewer was also not finished.

Because ten is a raid. Twenty-five is a **swarm**.

So the whole swarm went up. Through the Spire, the Plagueworks, the Crimson Hall and the Frostwing Halls, twenty-five rats in black and gold, until the wind on the summit was the only thing left to fight.

Arthas waited where he always waits, on the throne the world built for him, Frostmourne cold in his hand.

He tried every trick he had. The ghouls and the horrors, the plague that leaps from rat to rat, the ground torn out from under them, the spirits drifting out of the dark, and finally the cold place inside his own blade. The first pulls threw the swarm back down the steps. Each time it climbed again, a little sharper, a little quieter, until the pull where not one rat was out of place.

Then the blade that had drunk a thousand souls met the Ashbringer, and Frostmourne broke. The souls inside it poured out like a dam bursting, and the swarm went in right behind them.

The Lich King fell to one knee at the top of the world, under twenty-five pairs of red eyes.

*"There must always be a Lich King,"* someone said, a long time ago.

Fine. But tonight the throne answers to the Rats.

And as the old rite of the Sewer demands, twenty-five rats sat down on the fallen king, stood up, sat down, and stood up again.

*So it is written.*
```

## Notes

- **Event:** first guild **ICC 25** Lich King kill, **Mon 12 Oct 2026**, after a few good tries. Written
  ahead on 09 Oct with versions for each outcome; the post uses D.
- **Angle:** the callback to the 10-man Kingslayers ("ten is a raid, twenty-five is a swarm"). Lore anchors:
  Arthas, Frostmourne breaking against Tirion's Ashbringer, the souls pouring out, Bolvar's "there must
  always be a Lich King". It ends on the Sewer's sit/stand rite, the guild's tradition from the 10-man kill.

  spirits drifting out of the dark", "the cold place inside his own blade" stand in for the mechanics.
- **No @mentions** in the clean version on purpose: 25 names won't fit in 2000 characters. Version C and
  the hero line name a few; take the `<@ID>` from each rat's character sheet.
