## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# 🔧 The Butler at the End of the World

Every rat who has stood on the Frozen Throne knows the moment. The king grows tired of fighting. He raises his blade, and the whole roof dies at once. No tricks. No dodging. Twenty-five rats on the ice, and silence.

Then the old king inside the sword, the father Arthas murdered, reaches out from the dark and pulls them all back up.

Most rats stand up confused. **<@1295762889730297917>** stood up and looked down.

Every piece of his armor was red. The plate cracked, the shield split, the crown hanging on by a rivet. The Rat King had died so many times that night that his gear had simply given up. He was about to tank the Lich King in what was, technically, scrap.

Any other rat would have panicked. Grunho did something no rat in the Sewer had ever thought to do on that roof.

He called the butler.

A little iron servant clanked onto the ice at the top of the world, bowed politely to the Lich King, and started fixing things. Hammer. Rivet. Polish. Three seconds later the Rat King's plate was whole again, and he turned back to the throne like nothing had happened.

The swarm stared. The Lich King stared. Somewhere inside Frostmourne, a thousand souls asked who brings a butler to the end of the world.

The Rats do. The Rats always come prepared. Eventually.

*So it is written.*
```

## Notes

- **Event:** Lich King fight, after the king's raid-wide kill at the end and the resurrection. Grunho came
  back with all his gear broken (red) and put down a **Jeeves** repair bot right after the rez to repair
  before the final push. Written 13 Oct 2026. Which night/raid isn't stated in the post.
- **Lore:** the rez comes from **King Terenas's spirit** (Arthas's father, inside Frostmourne), not from
  Arthas himself, so the post says "the old king inside the sword, the father Arthas murdered".
- **NO spell names:** "raises his blade, and the whole roof dies at once" stands in for the mechanic.
  Jeeves is called "the butler" (that's what he is). "Twenty-five rats" assumes the 25-man; change to
  "Ten rats" if it was a 10.
- **Callback:** "had died so many times that night" plays on Grunho the charger (`lich-king-10-kill.md`,
  `grunho-and-the-red-tide.md`). Only Grunho is pinged.
- **Art:** `images/lore/Grunho Butler.png`, set in the `ART` map in
  `scripts/build-chronicles.py`.
