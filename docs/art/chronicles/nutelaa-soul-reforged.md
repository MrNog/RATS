# ❄️ A Soul Reforged ❄️

For years, **Nutelaa** walked the dark path of the Warlock — commanding demons, wielding shadow and flame, leaving ruin in her wake across every raid the Rats have ever set foot in.

But the sewer calls for more than shadow.

It calls for frost.

Without hesitation, without being asked, she shed her dark pact. Dismissed her demons one last time. And stepped into the cold.

From the ashes of the Warlock...

A Death Knight was born.

Twin axes in hand, frost runes burning blue, she now walks a harder path — and she walks it for us.

🧊 **Nutelaa** has risen. Frost Death Knight of the Rats.

May Icecrown tremble at what approaches.
