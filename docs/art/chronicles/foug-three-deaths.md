## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# ⚔️ The Three Deaths of Foug

Foug was born to the Light. In the first age he bore the Paladin's hammer, and it served him —
until he crossed **Gathlock**, and the Light could not save him. He fell. The tome does not soften it: one stood, and one did not.

What rose from that field was no zealot. Foug laid down the hammer and took up the bow,
and when the Rats asked why, he did not speak of glory. *"The easiest and quickest road,"* he called it — a hunter's path, a way to march to war without ceremony. Yet he did not walk it alone: at his side hunted a great beast he named **@Grunho**, and the two were seldom seen apart.

But easy roads do not last, and Foug was never meant to end as a passing arrow in the dark.
The bow is hung upon the wall now, the beast loosed to the wild. In blackened plate he rises a third time — a **Death Knight** of the Unholy, twin green blades cold in his claws, the dead stirring at his heel.

Three lives. One Rat. Still Scottish. Still Warchief.

*So it is written.*
```
