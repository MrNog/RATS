## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# 💃 A Dance for the King

Tonight the Rats climbed to the very top of the Citadel, to the Frozen Throne itself, where the wind cuts like a blade and the King of the Dead waits in his ice.

Every Rat had a task. The healers held their Light ready. The blades stood in their lines. And the tank, **<@1245098422705590316>**, the hammer of judgement, the same Rat who once heard "all" in the Crimson Hall and pulled the entire hall, had one job: stand before the King and keep his cold eyes on him.

The King rose from his throne. The raid charged.

Mongoloide danced.

Not a battle-step. Not a feint. A full, slow, swaying dance on the frozen platform, hammer forgotten at his feet, shield lying in the snow. Hips. Shoulders. A little spin.

The healers looked at each other. The blades looked at each other. And one voice asked what the whole raid was thinking:

*"Is... is Mongo AFK?"*

He did not answer. He was busy.

The scholars of the Sewer are divided. One school holds that he simply walked away and left his Rat dancing alone at the end of the world. The other school, larger and louder, believes it was strategy: that after so many nights of hitting the Lich King with a hammer, Mongoloide decided to try a different approach.

Charm.

Arthas, who has watched armies kneel and kingdoms freeze, stood before his throne and watched a rat shake his hips at him. Frostmourne dipped an inch. For the first time in all his long cold reign, the Lich King did not know what to do.

He was not seduced.

But he was, the Chronicles must admit, a little bit confused.

What happened to the raid after that, the Chronicles decline to record.

*So it is written.*
```
