## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# 🧪 Grunho and the Red Tide

High in the frozen citadel, in the laboratory where the mad one works, there is a thing that hunts.

It has no face to strike and no throat to open. It is a red tide in a glass-strewn hall, and it chooses
one rat out of all the rats, and it comes for that one and no other. The law of the thing is old and
simple, and every Rat who has ever set foot on that filthy floor knows it by heart: **you do not meet it.
You run, and you run alone, and you keep the rest of the world behind you.**

**<@1295762889730297917>** has never in his life run from anything.

He saw it turn. He saw it choose. And the Rat King did what the Rat King has done at every door, every
bridge and every wall the Rats have ever held — he set his shoulder, he picked his ground, and he
*charged* to meet it.

It was, the tome must record, magnificent. For perhaps a heartbeat and a half.

The red tide did not care that he was a king. It did not care about the crown, or the shield, or the
long red cloak, or the twenty-four other rats standing in a tidy little cluster directly behind him
exactly where he had told them to stand. It took him, and then — being a tide — it took everything he
was standing in front of.

The laboratory was quiet after that. The mad one above did not even look up.

They speak of it still. Not as a defeat. As a **blast**.

Let it be written plainly, so that no future rat mistakes the lesson: the Rat King has held every wall
worth holding. He has held doors that should not have held. He will hold them again.

He has, however, been formally excused from holding back a puddle.

*So it is written.*
```

## Notes

- **Event:** on the Professor's fight in Icecrown, the red ooze fixated on Grunho (GM, main raid tank,
  Prot Warrior). The mechanic wants the target to run it away from the group; he charged it instead,
  next to the stack, and the raid wiped. Universally remembered as a blast.
- **Angle:** the joke lands on the **mechanic and the moment**, never on Grunho being bad — his charging
  a thing that hunts him is played as completely in character for the rat who has never retreated from
  anything, and the raid dies because they trusted him, which is flattering. Same rule as the Exile
  chronicle: do not rewrite this as "the king griefed the raid".
- **Voice:** Loremaster — third person, epic tome prose, `@everyone` header, `*From the Chronicles of the
  Sewer...*`, `## ` title, italic seal. Comic register sits between the Exile's dry understatement and an
  outright roast; the punch is the last line's mock-formal exemption, not a gag.
- **NO spell names** (user rule) — no ooze/ability/boss names. Lore anchors only: the frozen citadel, the
  mad one (the Professor, unnamed), the laboratory, the red tide.
- **@mention:** replace `<@1295762889730297917>` with Grunho's real Discord ID so it pings him. (Copied
  from the Exile chronicle — verify before posting.)
- **Art:** Template E lore moment, Grunho's locked look at FULL kit — deliberately the inverse of the
  Exile piece (shield up not down, crown worn not set aside, charging not seated) per the ONE rule. Set
  in the laboratory where it happened. The raid are distant upright silhouettes so the frame reads as
  the instant *before* the wipe.
- **Content-safety:** the charge is a defiant advance, never an engulfing or a death; the ooze is
  explicitly a shapeless tide rather than a creature, and explicitly not blood; nobody is shown falling.
  Post-safe.
