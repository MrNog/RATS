# The Whole Room — RATS Chronicle

Heroic ICC 10, the weekly **Blood Quickening**: 30 minutes from the Crimson Hall to save Infiltrator
Minchar, which means killing the Blood Prince Council AND Blood-Queen Lana'thel inside the timer. The call
was "fast, pull it all" — meaning one pack at a time, no slow CC'ing (normally: stun one, sheep one, kill
them nearly one by one). Mongoloide, tanking, heard "all" and pulled every pack in the hall at once,
10+ Darkfallen. Wipe. Loremaster voice for the **Lore poster**. Happened BEFORE
[`blood-quickening-the-flames.md`](blood-quickening-the-flames.md), same night.

## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# 🩸 The Whole Room

Deep in the Crimson Hall, a spy named Minchar hangs in chains, waiting for the Blood Queen to finish him. The Rats were given half an hour. Thirty minutes to cut through her court, kill her princes, kill the queen, and carry him out.

Thirty minutes is not long in the Crimson Hall.

On a normal night the Rats clear those halls the careful way. A stun on one noble. A sheep on another. The archmage held, the tactician held, and the pack taken apart one vampire at a time, like a lock picked pin by pin.

There was no time for locks tonight.

**<@146677218280603648>** gave the order: *"Be quick. Pull everything."*

Every rat in the raid understood what that meant. One pack. Then the next. No sheep, no stuns, no patience — just speed.

Every rat but one.

**<@1245098422705590316>** raised his shield, looked down the length of the Crimson Hall, and heard the word *everything*. And Mongoloide is a rat who takes his orders seriously.

He did not pull the pack.

He pulled **the hall.**

Nobles. Blood knights. Archmages. Tacticians. Fiends. Ten of them, then twelve, then more — the whole court of the San'layn turning at once and coming down the red carpet like a wave of velvet and teeth.

For one long second the raid simply watched it arrive.

Then the healers did the math. Then the mages did the math. Then nobody had to do math anymore, because the floor of the Crimson Hall was covered in Rats.

Somewhere above them, the Blood Queen smiled. Minchar's clock kept ticking.

*He was told to pull everything. Nobody can say he didn't.*

*So it is written.*
```
