## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# ⚖️ The Five-Day Exile of the Rat King

There came a day when **<@1295762889730297917>** — he who has held the line at the front of every wall the Rats
ever stood behind — went down into a lesser dark with strangers at his back.

He was a shield-bearer. He had always been a shield-bearer. But the axe that fell from that hollow was no
shield-bearer's axe, and the Rat King has never pretended to be a humble creature.

So he did the honourable thing. **He spoke first.**
Before the roll, before the axe was even cold, he told them plainly: *I am taking this one as a warrior takes
it, not as a guardian.* No trickery. No silence. No hand slipped into another rat's satchel while the torches
were low.

And the dice fell, and they fell for him.

It was then that the Wardens came.

Not the Scourge. Not the Legion. Something colder and infinitely more petty — the faceless ones who watch
from outside the world, who do not raid, who have never held a wall, who read a single line of a report and
render judgement without ever once asking what was said aloud before the roll.

**Five days**, they decreed. Five days cast out of the world.

The Rat King did not argue. Kings do not argue with clerks.
He hung the tower shield on the wall, set the iron crown down beside it, and sat out the sentence in the dark
with the patience of something that knows it will still be here long after the Wardens have moved on to
bother somebody else.

The axe is still his. The ruling changed nothing but the calendar.

Five days is nothing. He has held doors longer than that.

*So it is written.*
```
