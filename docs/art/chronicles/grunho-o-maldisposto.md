## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# 🥽 Despicable Grunho

Every great villain of Azeroth keeps a lair, a scheme, and a scowl. **<@1295762889730297917>** kept all three,
and he never once asked for followers.

They came anyway.

They came in brass goblin goggles and blue work dungarees, a whole swarm of them, and they would not leave.
He growled at them. They saluted. He told them to go home. *They followed him to* **Icecrown**.

Nothing pleased him. Not the pulls, not the pace, not the henchman dangling from the end of his scarf.
He grumbled at the healers and glared at the damage dealers. When one of them stood in the fire, he said
nothing at all, and that was worse.

The swarm did not care. Where he walked, they ran after him. Where he stood, they stood behind him.
They brought him a wheel of cheese as tribute and he did not say thank you. So they brought him another.

No one in the Sewer can quite explain it. Other lords buy loyalty with gold, or speeches, or kindness.
The Rat King earned it by walking to the front of every wall, raising the **tower shield**, and never once stepping aside.

They do not follow him because he is kind. **He is not kind.**
They follow him because when the dark comes, he is already standing in front of it.

And somewhere behind the scowl, though he will never say it,
the Rat King counts every goggled head before the pull. *Every one.*

*Bad temper. Best tank.*

*So it is written.*
```

  because he is always the one standing in front. Source: the "Despicable Grunho" parody poster

- **Pings:** only Grunho. The henchmen are the swarm in general, nobody named.
