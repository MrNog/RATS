## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# 🔥 Ratorgina "Suki" Roedriguez, Queen of the Forge

Deep under Blackrock Mountain, the Dark Iron dwarves kept slaves at their forges. One of them was a rat with charcoal fur, amber eyes and hips the overseers kept "accidentally" staring at. She learned the anvil better than her masters. Then one night the forges went quiet, and so did three overseers. They died smiling. Nobody in the Sewer asks how.

She walked into the Sewer with a hammer on her shoulder and one question: *"Who here breaks the most... equipment?"*

Every rat raised a paw.

Her name is **Ratorgina Roedriguez**, but nobody in the Sewer calls her that. To the swarm she is **Suki**. Ask any rat why and he just smiles and looks at the floor.

She runs the forge on two shifts.

**By day** she fixes your gear. Plate, shields, blades, whatever you broke on the last boss. Firm hands, hot iron, no complaints.

**By night** she fixes your *other* gear. The forge door closes, the bellows keep pumping, and the hammering goes on until sunrise. Rats leave walking funny, with their durability back at 100%.

She takes no orders, only commissions. She charges **<@1295762889730297917>** double, "because he breaks it double, and he always asks for the full service."

The King pays. The King always pays. The King has been seen visiting the forge with nothing broken at all.

They say on the Frozen Throne, when the Rat King called a little iron butler to fix his gear instead of her, Suki heard about it before the raid was back in Orgrimmar. The butler has not been seen since. The King limped for a week.

He has never looked happier.

🐀 *The swarm bleeds. Suki polishes.* 🔨
```
