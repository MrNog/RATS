## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# ❄️ The Gem That Flew

As her strength failed, **Sindragosa** chose the first rat for the ice,
and her mark fell on **<@245958230117842954>**, priest of the Sewer.

The law of the mark is old and simple. *Walk away from your kin.*
Where the marked one stands the tomb will rise, and it will take everyone close enough to touch.

So the priest walked, alone across the cold floor, the raid safely far away. No law was broken.
There was only one small problem: the priest was now standing behind the dragon.

The **Frost Queen** did not even turn her head. Her tail came around like the wall of a falling keep
and struck the priest clean off the ground.

High over the **Frostwing Halls** flew **<@245958230117842954>**, robes streaming, the mark still burning.
For one moment the Rats looked up in wonder. Then they understood the direction of travel.

The priest came down in the exact middle of the raid.

**The ice rose.**

Sixteen tombs stood where the Rats had been. Above them the dragon spoke, soft as snowfall:
*"Can you feel the cold hand of death upon your heart?"*

From inside the ice came the voice of **<@199995741970759680>**, a **death knight** who had been having a very good night.

*"My parse!"*

A moment passed. The voice came again, quieter.

*"Damn you."*

The Sewer still remembers the mage who carried a tomb to his tank. That rat got it wrong.
This one got it right, and was *thrown*.

Since that night the priests of the Sewer are taught a second law of the mark:

*Never stand behind the dragon.*

*So it is written.*
```
