*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# 🔥 The Flames Stay With You

The clock was still running. Minchar was still in chains. And the Rats had made it — past the hall, past the princes, all the way to the throne of the Blood Queen herself, on **heroic**.

She was almost done. **Thirty percent.** The whole raid stood packed together under her, shoulder to shoulder, because that is how you survive the Queen: her pact chains rats together, and chains only break when the rats stand close. Ten rats, one tight knot. Nobody moves.

Except the one who has to.

Every so often the Queen marks a rat with her shadows. The mark is simple. It burns at your feet, and it follows you. And the law of that fight is simpler still: **you take your fire somewhere else.** You run. Away from the knot, away from your friends, and you leave the flames where they can't hurt anyone.

The shadows found **<@323497935210020866>**.

And Rellik — High Warlord, Warchief of the Rats, a blade that has cut down kings — was busy. The Queen was at thirty percent. His sword was singing. The numbers were beautiful.

He did not run.

He stood in the very center of the raid, swinging that great golden blade with both hands, and the fire pooled beneath him. Then it spread. Then it was under the healers. Then the tanks. Then everyone.

For a few seconds it was almost peaceful — ten rats, perfectly stacked, perfectly together, perfectly on fire.

The Blood Queen did not even have to bite anyone.

Thirty percent. So close you could taste it.

*The chains say stand together. The fire says stand alone. Learn the difference.*

*So it is written.*
