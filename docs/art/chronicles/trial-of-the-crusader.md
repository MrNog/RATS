*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# ⚔️ THE RATS ANSWER THE CALL — TRIAL OF THE CRUSADER

**Today, the gate opens. 🐀**

**Tirion Fordring** raised the Argent Crusade upon the ice of Icecrown and built a coliseum in the Lich King's own shadow — and there he asks every would-be champion one question before they dare march on the Frozen Throne: **are you worthy?**

Today he asks it of the **Rats**.

And now, out of every grate and shadow beneath this coliseum, **the swarm rises again.** At the front stand the hardest of the pack, blades lit and shields raised. Behind them — **all of us.** **Garrosh** himself leans forward on his seat to watch.

They used to laugh at the gutter-rats. They stopped laughing somewhere between Naxxramas and Ulduar.

Because beyond that gate the trial is waiting. The **beasts of Northrend** paw the sand. The pit-lord **Jaraxxus** coils in his portal. The **twin Val'kyr** take their marks, and far below, the traitor **Anub'arak** presses his ear to the floor — listening for the tread of fools.

**He is going to hear us coming.** 💀

No crown. No bloodline. Just teeth, steel, and a whole swarm that refuses to kneel.

So sharpen your fangs and get to the gate. **This is the night the coliseum learns our name.**

The Crusade asks who is worthy to march on the Lich King.

🔥 **TONIGHT, THE SEWER ANSWERS.** 🔥

For the Horde. For the cheese. **LONG LIVE RATS.** 🐀🧀

## Notes

### Real WoW lore (keep it accurate)
- The **Trial of the Crusader** sits inside the **Argent Tournament**: **Tirion Fordring** and the
  **Argent Crusade** built the **Crusaders' Coliseum** on the ice of **Icecrown** as a proving-ground —
  to find champions worthy to march on the **Lich King** in Icecrown Citadel.
- Encounter order, all named in the post: **Beasts of Northrend** (Gormok, Acidmaw & Dreadscale, Icehowl)
  → **Lord Jaraxxus** (an eredar/pit-lord accidentally summoned by Wilfred Fizzlebang) → the **Faction
  Champions** (Horde vs Alliance proving bout — "champion after champion sent home humbled") → the
  **Twin Val'kyr**, Eydis Darkbane & Fjola Lightbane (light and dark) → **Anub'arak**, the traitorous
  crypt lord who **bursts up through the coliseum floor** for the final fight.
- **Horde POV:** **Garrosh Hellscream** presides over the Horde side of the stands (Varian Wrynn for the
  Alliance) — which is why the render is hung with **red Horde banners**. Correct for a Horde guild.
