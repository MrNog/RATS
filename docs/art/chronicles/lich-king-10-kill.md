# Kingslayers — The Frozen Throne Falls — RATS Chronicle

The swarm's **10-man Lich King kill** and the **Kingslayer** title. A smooth clear of the citadel, then a
long hard night on the king himself, past the usual 3-hour call, past midnight. On the "one more" try,
in the king's last phase, Grunho charged him (again, see the Red Tide), never saw the frozen orb, bumped
it with his shoulder and wiped the raid. The raid said "it's fine, one
more", and the next pull killed him. After the kill the ten did the guild's sit-stand rite on the
fallen king. **After-action victory**: one post, two art prompts (funny + the story). Loremaster voice
for the officer **Lore poster**. Real WoW lore anchors, **no spell names**, Discord markdown, ≤2000 chars.

## Notes

### What happened (from the user)
- **Goal of the night:** kill the Lich King (10-man). The clear through the citadel's wings was **smooth**.
- **The king was hard.** The raid adjusted mechanics over several tries; everyone was excited about how
  close they were. Normally the guild raids **~3 hours and calls it around midnight**; this time they kept
  going past midnight — "just one more try".
- **The wipe:** on that try, in the king's **last phase** with him almost dead, **Grunho** (GM, main tank,
  Warrior) was full of rage and **charged the Lich King, "again"**. The "again" is a callback to his
  charge into the red ooze in `grunho-and-the-red-tide.md`. He never noticed the frozen orb, **bumped it
  with his shoulder**, it exploded and wiped the raid.
- **The turn:** instead of calling it, the raid said *"it's ok Grunho, we all make mistakes, one more"*.
  Everyone played their best and the next pull **killed the Lich King** → title **Kingslayer**.
- **The rite:** after the kill everyone did the guild's **sit / stand / sit / stand** on the Lich King's
  body. The guild does this a lot when someone dies; here it's played as "the old rite of the Sewer".
- **The ten Kingslayers:** Grunho, Okanor, Rellik, Kobee, Onetreeheals, Shmurda, Lecoque, Tchilly,
  Cryptwall, Yahmom. They're named in the post (a deliberate break from the no-names chronicle
  convention, at the user's request: "immortalized in RATS history").
