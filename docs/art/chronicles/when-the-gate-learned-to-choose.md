# ⚔️ When the Gate Learned to Choose

The Fangs were never a prize. They were a solution to a scheduling problem.

In the old halls the difficulty was chosen at the door. A raid went in hard or it went in soft, and that choice bound every rat inside it for the whole night. But the swarm had never wanted one thing. Some rats came to grind the hard modes until the mechanics broke. Others came to clear, to gear, to farm and go to bed. Both were right. Neither could have the night.

So the Warchief drew a tier — the Fangs — and it meant something plain: these are the rats who signed for the hard way. Not better. *Willing.* It let the guild field a hard-mode roster without dragging anyone into a wall they never asked for, and it let the rest raid in peace. It was a workaround, and it worked.

Then came Icecrown.

And Icecrown does not ask at the door. It asks at every boss. The same raid, the same night, the same rats — and the choice made fresh at each gate. Hard where the swarm has the appetite for it. Straight through where it doesn't. One roster, deciding as it goes.

The problem the Fangs were built to solve simply stopped existing.

So the tier came down, for the reason any good tool is put away: the job is done. No roster to keep separate, no second ladder to tend, no rat sorted by which difficulty they prefer. The willing and the steady raid the same night now, and Icecrown sorts it boss by boss.

Three ranks remain: the sewer, the raiders, and the Warchiefs who lead them.

The gate chooses now. We just show up.

*Set down in the Chronicles of the Sewer.*
