# ⚖️ Addendum: The Pardon

On the fifth day, with six hours left of it, the word came down from the windowless room: *a review has been conducted. The judgement is overturned.*

Six hours. Somewhere a Warden had finally read the second line of the report — the one that was there the whole time — and unlocked the door with the air of a man doing somebody a great favour.

**Grunho** said nothing. He took the tower shield down off the wall, set the iron crown back where it belongs, and picked up the axe that was always his.

Four days and eighteen hours. The clerks always get there in the end, right about the time it stops mattering.

*So it is written.*
