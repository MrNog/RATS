```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# 🏆 A TRIBUTE TO INSANITY

**Tirion built a coliseum to find out who is worthy to march on the Lich King. Tonight ten rats walked in to answer him. 🐀**

The beasts of Northrend came first, out of the cold and the deep sand. Then a gnome reached past his grasp and let a **pit-lord of the Burning Legion** onto the floor. Then the Crusade sent living champions — the only enemy in that place that could think. Every door opened, and every door closed behind us. 💀

Then the **Val'kyr** came on Arthas' errand, and we understood he had been watching the whole time.

When the trial was nearly done, **the Lich King took the floor out from under us.** Down in the dark beneath the arena **Anub'arak** was waiting — his own general, sent up through the ice to end us before we ever reached his gates.

**He is still down there. We are not.** 🕷️

The Crusade counts. Fifty times that coliseum was willing to watch us fall, and the whole arena counts with it. **It never got to start.**

So they brought out the chest they took off the **Black Knight** — Arthas' own spy, planted in the tournament and put in the ground for it — and the Crusade handed his spoils to ten rats from the sewer. 🏆

They have a name in that place for those who go in and never once go down. It is not meant kindly.

**We were insane. And we won.** 🐀🧀
```

## Notes

### What's real (from the user)
- **First full ToGC 10-man clear — 5/5 Heroic**, and **done with guild only** ("with guild"), no pugs.
  That is the emotional core and the deliberate contrast with the previous Heroic night, which was
  short-handed and needed two strays. The post is built on that turn: *came short, came back whole.*
- **Finished at 50/50 attempts — the achievement `A Tribute to Insanity`.** ToGC gives the raid **50 tribute
  attempts** for the lockout and burns one on every wipe; the guild ended with all 50 intact. This is the
  real headline, and it is told as *lore, never as a number* — the post says the Crusade "never got to
  start" counting. No digits, no wipe count, no `5/5` (see the SHORT AND PURE LORE note).
- **Tier names are SIZE-DEPENDENT — do not mix them up:**
  - **10-man**, 50/50 → **A Tribute to Insanity** ← *this run*
  - **25-man**, 50/50 → A Tribute to Immortality
  There is no 10-man "Immortality"; Insanity is the top tier on 10-man. (An earlier draft of this file used
  Immortality and had to be corrected.)
- **All five encounters killed in the run** — a complete clear, so the post can brag full-clear, unlike
  `heroic-toc-first-kill.md` which was explicitly NOT a full-clear brag.
- Kept **10-man** out of the prose (guild convention avoids size/mechanics talk in chronicles) — it lives in
  the file title and the art (a band of exactly ten). "Grand Crusader" already signals Heroic.

### Real WoW lore (keep it accurate)
- **Trial of the Grand Crusader** = the Heroic version of the Trial of the Crusader, Tirion Fordring / the
  Argent Crusade's proving ground on the ice of Icecrown, built to find champions worthy to march on the
  **Lich King**. Horde POV throughout.
- Encounter order, all five named in sequence: **Beasts of Northrend** (Gormok the Impaler → the twin
  jormungar Acidmaw & Dreadscale → Icehowl) → **Lord Jaraxxus** (eredar pit-lord of the Burning Legion,
  loosed when the gnome Wilfred Fizzlebang overreaches) → **Faction Champions** (living PvP-style enemy
  players — the "enemy that thinks") → **Val'kyr Twins** (Fjola and Eydis, light/dark, Arthas' servants) →
  **Anub'arak** (the crypt lord of Azjol-Nerub, raised by the Lich King; the arena floor collapses into the
  nerubian ruins beneath for the final fight).
- The floor genuinely breaks open for Anub'arak — that's why the art is a pit composition, not arena sand.
- **The tribute chest is the Black Knight's.** He is a Scourge agent who infiltrates the Argent Tournament,
  is exposed, killed, and returns through the trial's storyline as undead. The Crusade seizes his spoils and
  awards them to the champions who prove worthy — so the reward is loot taken off one of Arthas' own spies
  and handed over by the people fighting him. That is why the post names him in the tribute beat: it ties
  the prize back to Arthas, who has been the through-line since the Val'kyr.
- The 50 attempts read in-fiction as **the Crusade's patience, spent in public** — the coliseum stands are
  packed and every fall is witnessed. That is the image the tribute beat uses ("the whole arena counts with
  it"), which is how the achievement gets told without quoting the counter.

### Craft notes
- **After-action.** Past tense throughout, closing on the guild putting its name on the list of those
  worthy to march on the Lich King.
- **SHORT AND PURE LORE — the governing constraint.** The user's call: *"make small, no need to say 0 wipes,
  make a lore story"*. So: no `5/5` in the title, no kill count, no wipe count, no roster size boast, no
  "ALL RATS START TO FINISH" chant. Bosses are named as *story beats*, not ticked off a list — the Beasts get
  half a sentence, the Faction Champions get their one good line ("the only enemy that could think"), and the
  weight all lands on Anub'arak. If this file is expanded later, expand the *lore*, not the stats.
- **The Arthas twist carries the ending.** The last two trials are canonically NOT part of Tirion's audition
  — the Val'kyr are Arthas' servants and he personally collapses the arena floor to send Anub'arak up and
  kill the finalists before they can reach Icecrown. So the post reframes the finale from "a last boss" to
  "the Lich King tried to end us himself and lost his general doing it", and the closing line answers it:
  he sent his best, his best is still in the ice. Lore-accurate and a much stronger finish than a boss list.
- **Sets up ICC.** Clearing the Trial is literally how a group earns the march on Icecrown Citadel, so the
  post ends on the list rather than on the kill — the next chronicle is the march north.
- **No individual names** (guild chronicle convention) — the swarm as one body. No boss counts, no 10/25
  tag, no game-mechanics talk.
- **NO spell names** (user rule) — weight from real Warcraft anchors only.
- **Voice:** Loremaster — `@everyone`, `*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*`, `# ` title, grim tome prose,
  guild flare (🐀🧀). Under 2000 chars for the poster.
- **Faction Champions** get the "enemy that thinks" line — the one encounter that isn't a monster, which is
  what makes it the interesting beat in an otherwise beast-and-demon list.
- **Art inverts the previous piece on purpose:** that one was a *thin* line spread too wide; this one is a
  *packed* band of exactly ten with no gaps. Same coliseum, opposite silhouette — the guild-only clear read
  at a glance. The vast beaten spider-lord below supplies scale; warm gold from above beats the cold dead
  glow from below.
