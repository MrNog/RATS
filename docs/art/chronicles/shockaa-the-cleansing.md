## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# 🧪 The Cleansing

In the Plague Works the Professor waits behind his green table, and the Rats came to him as they always do — late, loud, and one healer short.

**<@269201538013069314>** was there in body. His hands were on the totems. His mind was somewhere else entirely.

The pull went anyway.

So **<@1295762889730297917>** did what kings do when no one else will: he walked to the table and drank the Professor's brew. It does not taste of victory. It tastes of sewer, and of regret, and of something that used to be a gnome. His fur fell out. His plate split. What stood up was an **Abomination** — and it got to work, eating the slime off the floor and chaining the oozes while the raid lived on whatever healing was left.

Then Shockaa came back.

He looked at the raid. He saw a grotesque, swollen, *unnatural* thing lurching among his allies — clearly a curse, clearly an affliction, clearly a raider in need of a healer.

And with the pure, clean light of a Shaman doing his duty, **he dispelled it.**

The Abomination was gone. Grunho stood in the puddles, a rat again, holding nothing. Somewhere behind his table the Professor laughed.

There was no speech. Grunho turned, walked back to the green table, and drank the shit potion **a second time.**

Shockaa healed beautifully for the rest of the fight. Nobody has thanked him.

*So it is written.*
```
