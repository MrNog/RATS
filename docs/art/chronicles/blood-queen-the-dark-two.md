# 🩸 The Dark Two

The Blood Queen does not kill you all at once. She takes her time. A bite here, a terror there, and one by one the Rats went down on the cold floor of her hall, until the hall was quiet and the Queen was still flying.

Eight rats on the floor. Two still standing.

Not the tanks. Not the healers. Not the heroes of the Light.

**<@209740087653564439>** — a Death Knight who reads his runes the way other rats read the weather, and who was not tanking anything that night.

And **<@940982602976858142>** — a Warlock who keeps his curses in a book and his demon on a short chain.

Two rats who had spent their whole lives studying the dark. And now they were the only thing left standing between the Queen and a full wipe.

Nobody on the floor said a word. Nobody breathed. Every rat in the raid sat there holding his heart in his hands — and, if we are honest, holding a few other things as well.

The Queen rose into the air. The air phase. The one that kills raids that are *full*. Shadows rained down on two rats.

Foug's ghoul did not stop. Franz's curses did not stop. The runes burned green, the shadows burned violet, and for twenty long seconds the darkness fought the darkness — and the darkness that belonged to the Rats was hungrier.

She came back down. They were still there.

And then the Blood Queen fell to the two least holy creatures in the guild.

The floor of the hall exploded in noise.

*The Light had left the room. It turned out we didn't need it.*

*So it is written.*
