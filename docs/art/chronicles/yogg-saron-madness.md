# 🩸 The Madness in the Brain of Yogg-Saron 🩸

It came in the second phase, as it always does — when the floor opened and the portals yawned wide, beckoning the bravest into the mind of the Old God itself.

And so the Warchief's Fangs went in. Down into the brain room, into the whispering dark, where the walls breathe and reason rots.

But the Fangs did not look at the tentacles. They looked back — through the rift, out onto the raid floor — and what they saw was death. Rat after rat, falling. A pile of the fallen growing higher with every breath.

The Sanity of the Fangs drained like blood from a wound.

The whispers grew louder. *Look what they've done. Look how they die without you.* And something in the Fangs — something tired, something laughing — agreed.

They came back through the portal not as saviours, but as servants of the madness. Eyes empty. Blades turned the wrong way.

And the Fangs fell upon their own.

It was not a wipe of failure. It was a wipe of comedy — the protectors of the guild, mind-broken, gleefully cutting down every last rat they had sworn to defend. The raid ended in laughter and ash.

Yogg-Saron won that night. But the legend was the Fangs', and it will be told for as long as rats remember how to laugh at their own ruin.

*— The Loremaster*
