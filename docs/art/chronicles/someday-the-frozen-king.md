*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# ❄️ Someday and the Frozen King

Sindragosa. Last phase. The Frost Queen was low, and the rats had done everything right.

Before the pull the raid leader asked once: "Does anyone not know what to do?"

Nobody spoke. Every rat was ready, or so the silence said.

Then the Frost Beacon landed on **<@857062920743550996>**.

The job was simple: walk to the middle and take the Ice Tomb there.

<@857062920743550996> went to the side.

"MIDDLE!" yelled the raid. "<@857062920743550996>, MIDDLE!"

<@857062920743550996> heard it, and <@857062920743550996> acted. He hit **Blink** and went forward, past the middle, right into the front, where the tank was standing.

**<@1295762889730297917>** had a moment to turn his head.

The tomb landed. Two blocks of ice rose side by side: one with a confused mage in it, and one with the Rat King, crown and shield and all, frozen in place in front of the dragon.

Sindragosa looked at her frozen tank. Then she looked at the raid with no tank.

**WIPE.**

The raid leader typed one line in chat:

> "So... nobody had questions?"

That broke the silence. Somebody snorted on voice, then somebody else, and within seconds the whole sewer was laughing: twenty-five rats lying dead on the ice, crying with laughter, while the Rat King thawed out with the look of a man planning a very long talk.

Nobody was angry. It was the best wipe of the night.

Since that night every pull starts the same way. The question is no longer "does anyone not know what to do?" It is: **"<@857062920743550996>, where is the middle?"**

*So it is written.*
