```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# 👑 Three Crowns

Most rats climb to the Frozen Throne once, if the cheese is kind, and go to bed proud.

<@209740087653564439> climbed it three times. In one night. Three different raids, three different jobs, one king, and the king lost every time.

**The first crown** came with the whole swarm. Twenty-five rats on the Frozen Throne, the guild's first, and Foug among them, twin green blades in the king's back as Frostmourne broke.

**The second crown** came with ten. Same Death Knight, new work: this time Foug stood *in front* of the king, took the blade on his own plate, and held him there until there was nothing left to hold. His ghoul chewed on what remained of the throne.

**The third crown** came with ten again, and with an old friend. Foug dug the Paladin out of the oldest chest in the Sewer, the armor nobody had seen in an age. He raised the shield of his first life and tanked the Lich King to his knees. The Light still remembered him.

Three climbs to the top of the world before the sun came up. Three times the blade that drank a thousand souls went quiet. And three times, as the old rite of the Sewer demands, Foug sat on the fallen king and stood up again. Sat. Stood. His knees have opinions now.

Most rats would be proud of one crown. Foug took three, and wears none of them. He has seen what thrones do to people.

Three raids. Three roles. One king, three times on the ice.

*So it is written.*
```
