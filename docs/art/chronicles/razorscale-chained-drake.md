# ⚒️ The Long Night of the Chained Drake ⚒️

Razorscale did not fall easily. She never does.

Bound in her pit by iron chains, the great proto-drake screamed fire across the Antechamber while the Watchers of Ulduar marched endlessly from the dark — iron dwarves without number, hammers raised, eyes like cold forge-light.

The Rats had not come merely to kill her. They had come for the harder thing — the deed the scribes call *achievement* — and that meant the dwarves had to die. All twenty-five of them, cut down amid the harpoons and the falling flame, in the narrow space between one breath of fire and the next.

The first wipe came fast. So did the second. And the third.

The pit filled with their dead and emptied again as they returned, and returned, and returned. Harpoons missed. A drake grounded too soon. A dwarf left standing when the timer bled out. Each failure a fresh bruise.

A lesser warband would have called the ID and gone to bed. The Rats did not.

They learned the rhythm of it — who held the line, who struck the chains, who counted the dwarves aloud in the chaos. Wipe by wipe the war tightened, until at last the twenty-fifth iron body hit the stone and Razorscale crashed down with it.

Exhausted. Repair-poor. Victorious.

The achievement was theirs — earned the only way the Rats ever earn anything. The hard way.

*— The Loremaster*
