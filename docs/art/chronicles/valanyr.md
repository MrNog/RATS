## Notes

### Real WoW lore (keep it accurate)
- **Val'anyr, Hammer of Ancient Kings** — the classic WotLK Ulduar legendary, a one-hand **healing** mace.
  In lore it was **forged by the titan-keepers** and wielded by the **kings of the earthen** (the
  titan-forged ancestors of the dwarves) deep beneath Ulduar.
- A tool of **restoration, not destruction** — it mended and protected. When the **curse of flesh** undid
  the earthen and their kingdom fell, the hammer was **shattered** and its power lay dormant.
- Reforging it: a champion gathers the **Fragments of Val'anyr** (they drop from Ulduar bosses) and the
  Hammer is **reforged whole** — canonically restored at/with the blessing of the titans' works in Ulduar.
- Fits Okanor perfectly: a **healer's** legendary for the guild's Holy Paladin Lightkeeper — a weapon that
  "makes whole" is exactly his character.

### Craft notes
- **The story is the guild achievement** — RATS finally landing a legendary mace. No "29/30", no "one
  short", no server/phase/game-mechanics talk. Pure triumph + real Warcraft legend.
- **Wielder:** Okanor "The Lightkeeper" (Holy Paladin, Warchief). See `../characters/okanor/okanor.md`
  for his locked look if you want matching hero-art.
- **Voice:** Horde, rat/cheese flavor, no "colleagues". Under 2000 chars for the Discord poster.
- **Content-safety:** framed as forging / restoration, no gore — safe for a companion render (Okanor
  raising the reforged Val'anyr, gold light in Ulduar's titan-bronze halls).
