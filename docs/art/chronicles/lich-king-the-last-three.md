## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# ⚔️ The Last Three

The Rats climbed to the Frozen Throne and pulled the king **once**. No wipes. No second try. And for a long while it was almost easy. The plan held. The ice held. The king bled slowly, as kings do.

Then came the last phase, and the throne remembered what it was.

The first tank fell. The second took his place, and fell. The king did not hurry. He never needs to. One by one the shields went down on the ice, and behind them the swarm began to thin — a rat here, a rat there, until the roof of the world was mostly silence and frost.

**Fifteen percent.** Three rats still standing.

**<@295517084417261578>** was never a tank. He is a warrior of the two-handed blade, built to break things, not to hold them. But there was no one else. So he stepped in front of the Lich King, raised his steel, and *took the king's attention* — and kept it.

**<@170571891784810498>**, the last blade in the raid, did not look at the bodies or the numbers. Poison, steel, the king's back. Again. Again.

And **<@146677218280603648>**, the last healer, poured every drop of Light he had left into one warrior who should never have been standing there.

Fourteen. Twelve. The king struck, and Shmurda did not fall. Eleven. The Light held.

**Ten.**

The Lich King stopped. The blade in his hand went still. And the throne that had swallowed a whole raid let go of the last three Rats standing on it.

Seven rats lay on the ice and watched it happen. Nobody spoke. Then everyone did.

*One pull. Three rats. Five percent. One king.*

*So it is written.*
```
