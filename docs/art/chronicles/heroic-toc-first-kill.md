## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# 🩸 TWO FALL IN THE COLISEUM — FIRST HEROIC NIGHT

**Tonight the sand ran red, and it was not ours. 🐀**

We walked into the **Crusaders' Coliseum** on the hard road — the **Heroic** trial, the one that does not forgive — and we walked in **short.** Raiders scattered to the wind, on leave and away, the pack thinned to the bone. So thin we opened the gate and let **two strays** run with the swarm to fill the line. Not one Rat flinched, and the strays held right alongside them.

**Tirion** opened the gate, and the **Beasts of Northrend** came. First **Gormok** the impaler. Then the great worms out of the deep, **Acidmaw** and **Dreadscale**. And last the mountain himself, **Icehowl**, who lowered his horns and charged the whole pack at once. A long, grinding hold — blades not lowering, healers running dry and refusing to fall — and when the last beast went down into the bloodied sand, **the swarm was still standing.** 💀

But the Coliseum was not done asking. Beyond the churned floor a second door burned open. A gnome named **Wilfred Fizzlebang** reached beyond his grasp, and through the portal stepped no servant — **Lord Jaraxxus**, pit-lord of the Burning Legion, a general who had never once feared a rat.

He met this swarm short-handed and bleeding. He scattered his fel-fire across the sand and called his mistresses out of the flame — and **the thin line held, and closed, and pulled him down too.** 🔥

Two Heroic kills in one night. Fewer of us than ever. **First Heroic night in the book — and it was a double.**

They built this coliseum to find who is worthy to march on the Lich King. Tonight, short-handed and unbroken, **the Sewer answered twice.**

🔥 **BEASTS DOWN. PIT-LORD DOWN. LONG LIVE RATS.** 🔥

For the Horde. For the cheese. 🐀🧀
```
