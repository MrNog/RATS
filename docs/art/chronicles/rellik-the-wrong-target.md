## Discord post — the chronicle (Lore poster)

```
@everyone

*From the Chronicles of the Sewer...*

# ⚔️ The Wrong Target

For a full turning of the moon the Rats went up the Citadel every single night, and every single night
**<@323497935210020866>** was there — greatsword in both hands, the High Warlord of the front line,
cutting a road through the Scourge for everyone behind him.

Then came the alt run, and a strange thought took him. He set the paladin aside, woke his death knight
from the cold, and said: *Tonight, I will be the one who holds them.*

The Rats cheered. The Rats should have asked questions.

High above the clouds the two great airships closed on each other, cannon roaring, and it fell to the
tank to do the one thing the tank does on that deck: leap the gap, land among the
enemy, and seize the dwarf lord at the rail with the grip of the dead. Hold him. Keep his eyes on you and only on you, so
he does not walk across and tear the raid apart.

Rellik leapt. Rellik landed. It was a beautiful jump.

And then Rellik turned his back on the dwarf lord entirely and started beating a mage.

On the Horde deck the raid watched the commander stand there, untouched, unbothered, free to do as he
pleased. The question went up through the cannon smoke:

*"So... nobody is holding the boss?"*

And from the far deck, calm as a sunrise, came the answer:

*"I'm already hitting the mage."*

It took the High Warlord a long moment to look up from the mage, then over at the dwarf waiting at the
edge of the ship, and finally understand what the whole raid had been trying to tell him.

He held the dwarf lord after that. And when the smoke cleared he gave his verdict on the matter, with
all the dignity of a month of nightly raiding behind him:

*"I've never tanked this. I don't know."*

A month. Every night. The airship, every night.

The mage, for the record, did not survive. Nobody has ever been so thoroughly hit.

*So it is written.*
```
