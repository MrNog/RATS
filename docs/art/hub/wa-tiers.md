# WeakAura tier cards — art for the four tier packs on the Addons page

Format J rules ([STYLE.md](../STYLE.md#j--hub-card-1610-no-text)), with one difference: the card prints the
tier number and the raid name over the **bottom** of the image, so the lower ~40% stays dark and simple.
Shown about 280×140 — one landmark, one rat, strong silhouette.

Save as `images/hub/wa-t7.png` … `wa-t10.png`. Until a file exists the card falls back to the raid banner.
Each prompt is complete on its own: copy it whole.

---

## T7 — Naxxramas → `images/hub/wa-t7.png`

```
Digital fantasy illustration, 16:9 landscape (1600x900), World of Warcraft cinematic concept art, dark-souls Horde style, painterly brushwork, ultra-detailed, subtle film-grain texture overlay.

Scene: the floating necropolis of Naxxramas hovering in the night sky above the frozen wastes of Dragonblight — a vast dark ziggurat of black stone and bone-spiked buttresses, its underside glowing a sickly plague-green. It fills the UPPER HALF of the frame, center.

Subject & pose: one anthropomorphic rat raider with EXACTLY ONE tail, one head, two arms — dark fur, whiskers, battle-worn black-and-gold Horde plate, a tattered cloak. Small in the frame, lower-left third, standing on a snowy rock ridge seen from BEHIND and slightly to the side, looking up at the necropolis. His RIGHT hand grips a single greatsword planted point-down in the snow; his LEFT hand hangs at his side. NO other weapons.

Composition: the necropolis is the star; the LOWER 40% of the frame is dark snow and shadowed ridge, simple and low-detail, for text to sit on. One clear silhouette of the rat against the green glow.

Render quality: CLEAN and crisp — clear cold air, NO floating dust motes, NO ember particles, NO speckled glowing dots, NO heavy haze or fog, NO bloom.

Color palette & lighting: plague-green from the necropolis as the single key light, rim-lighting the rat's shoulder and cloak; deep blue-black night everywhere else.

Do NOT include: any lettering, title, name, number, watermark or logo; two tails or any extra tail, extra or duplicated limbs/arms/heads, extra weapons, malformed hands, over-rotated joints, a floating or over-tilted head, cute/pretty/soft styling, pastel or pink colours, chubby build, undead bodies or corpses, floating particles, heavy haze, modern clothing, human faces, Alliance symbols, plain white background.
```
