# Zamorei — "The Tidecaller"

- **Class / spec:** **Restoration Shaman — healer** (MAIN)
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 11 Jul 2026 · profile-banner sweep (one banner per raider)
- **Toon name:** **Zamorei** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).
- **⭐ ELITE TIER — #5 HPS guild-wide (avg ~2,435).** One of the guild's top healers — give him scale.

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** deep sea-grey fur, prominent whiskers, hard weathered eyes lit **cold cyan**, clawed hands, a
  salt-bleached scar across the brow. Lean, hard, storm-beaten — an old sea-shaman. **Tough, never soft.**
- **Class read:** **Resto Shaman — the TIDE side.** His healing is **water and wave**: cold cyan, not gold.
  Where a paladin's Light is a hammer, his water is a **tide that drowns wounds shut**. Harsh, not gentle.
- **Armor:** heavy weathered sea-mail — dark blue-green scaled plate crusted with salt and barnacle,
  a mantle of grey-blue kraken-hide, bone fetishes and coral charms, a ragged sea-blue cloak, forearm wraps.
- **Weapon:** ONE **tide-staff** — driftwood-and-iron wound with kelp, crowned with a raw cyan tide-stone —
  gripped in ONE clawed hand. **Other hand held out, palm down, pulling the water up.** NO second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** **a great WAVE of cyan water rising and curling behind him** — a wall of tide he is
  calling up out of the ground, and **healing water spiralling around his outstretched hand**.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** sea-grey fur, dark blue-green mail, salt-white crust, kelp-brown, **cold cyan** tide glow.
  Class colour Shaman `#0070DD` (the strip tints to that blue — perfect match).
- **Key light:** the **cyan glow inside the rising wave**, lighting him from BEHIND and the SIDE — cold,
  aquatic, rippling light through water; deep storm-dark elsewhere.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

- **Character card (21:9):** staff planted on a wet reef, tide curling at his back.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text · EPIC):** **the rising tide** — one hand held OUT and PALM-DOWN, hauling a
  **towering wall of cyan water UP and CURLING over behind him**, staff braced in the other hand, cloak and
  fur soaked and streaming, his hard face lit from behind by the light inside the wave. He sits RIGHT,
  dark left third. (The only water/wave banner in the set.)
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** salt-scarred brow, cold cyan eyes, barnacled pauldron, water running.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** lower-center on a black reef, the wave towering up the whole frame.
- **Combat / lore moment:** driving a wall of tide across a field of advancing dark silhouettes.

## Notes / history

- **The two resto shamans are deliberately split:** **Zamorei = the TIDE — a towering WAVE, cold cyan,
  sea-mail, salt and kelp, staff.** **Fazcafe = the SPIRIT-HEALER — chain-heal arcs, ancestor spirits,
  totems, warm blue-white.** Never give Zamorei a chain-heal or Fazcafe a wave.
- Also distinct from Shockaa (Elemental, lightning) and Twihnz (Enhancement, dual-wield). Four shamans,
  four unmistakable identities.
- Per STYLE.md healers read **tough** — he is a storm-beaten sea-shaman, never a serene water-spirit.
