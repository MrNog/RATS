## Locked look (never changes between images)

- **Rat:** lean and wiry but hard — a grizzled elder-druid build, long sinewy arms; a **medium-long pointed
  snout with a grey-flecked muzzle**, deep-set eyes that glow **silver-white like starlight**, one ear
  ragged. Dark fur. Weathered, stern, wise-and-dangerous — never soft.
- **Armor / vestments:** layered **druidic robes** in deep forest-green and bark-brown, heavy and travel-worn,
  over a leather harness; a **bear-pelt mantle** across the shoulders; a hood thrown back. The robe hems and
  mantle are overgrown — **dark thorned brambles, ivy and night-blooming flowers** (deep crimson wild roses,
  moon-white blossoms) growing out of the cloth. Flowers read as **wild and thorny**, never pretty.
- **Head:** **antlers** grown through the hood — gnarled, wrapped in vine, a few tiny blossoms on them.
- **Weapon(s):** ONE gnarled wooden **staff** (living wood, roots at the base, a crescent-moon fork at the
  top cradling a small star-fire light) in one hand. The other hand is **bare claws**. NO other weapons.
- **Signature detail:** a **spectral feral spirit-cat made of starlight and emerald nature-energy** prowling
  at his side — silver-blue star-glow body with emerald veins, glowing eyes, bared fangs. It is the feral
  spec made visible; always show it. Above/around him: **falling starfall streaks** (a few clean bright
  streaks, not speckle).
