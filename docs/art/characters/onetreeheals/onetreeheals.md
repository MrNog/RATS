# Onetreeheals — "The Last Standing Tree"

- **Class / spec:** Restoration Druid — healer (MAIN)
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 11 Jul 2026 · profile-banner sweep (one banner per raider)
- **Toon name:** **Onetreeheals** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).
- **🔥 #1 HPS IN THE GUILD (avg ~3,256 — the highest of every healer).** This is a **flagship banner**:
  the most epic healer composition in the set. His name is literally the concept — **the ONE TREE still
  standing.** Give it colossal scale. He is the guild's greatest healer and the art must say so at a glance.

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** slate-grey fur, prominent whiskers, cold unblinking eyes lit **pale spring-green**, clawed hands —
  tall, gaunt, immovable. The rat who is still standing when everything else has fallen. **Hard, never soft.**
- **Class read:** **Resto Druid** — the LAST tree left. His healing reads as **bark and stone**: the green
  light armours people, it doesn't cuddle them.
- **Armor:** heavy druidic plate-of-bark — literal **slabs of dark treebark and grey stone** fused into
  layered armor, splintered and gouged from hits it has absorbed; a mantle of dead leaves and iron thorns.
- **Weapon:** ONE **great root-staff** — a thick trunk-like staff, taller than him, gripped in **BOTH hands**
  and held **UPRIGHT / planted like a standard**. NO second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** a **canopy of pale-green light** blooming from the crown of his staff — a wide,
  spreading healing aura shaped like a tree's crown arcing over him.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** slate-grey, dark treebark brown, dead-leaf amber, **pale spring-green** healing glow, iron.
  Class colour Druid `#FF7C0A` (UI only — the render glow is PALE GREEN).
- **Key light:** the **pale-green canopy above the staff crown**, spilling DOWN over him like light through
  leaves; cold dark ambient behind.

  canopy of light ABOVE.** Liyondry kneels and pulls roots from the GROUND. Never mix them up.
