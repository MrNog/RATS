## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** ash-brown fur going white at the muzzle and brow, prominent whiskers, hard old eyes lit
  **spirit blue-white**, clawed hands, ritual **white war-paint** streaked across the face. Wiry, grizzled,
  unbending — a veteran spirit-talker. **Tough, never soft.**
- **Class read:** **Resto Shaman — the SPIRIT side.** His healing is **ancestral**: chain-heal arcs leaping
  through the air and the ghosts of dead ancestors standing at his back. Blue-white, cold, solemn.
- **Armor:** layered ritual mail-and-hide — dark iron scale under a heavy mantle of grey wolf-pelt, a
  **bone-and-feather headdress** (worn back off the face so it reads), ancestor-totem charms and rattling
  bone strings across the chest, a frayed spirit-blue sash.
- **Weapon:** ONE **totem-carved shaman mace** — heavy dark wood and iron, an ancestor-face carved into the
  head, glowing blue-white — gripped in ONE clawed hand and **held UP and out to the side**. NO second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** **CHAIN-HEAL** — a bright blue-white **arc of spirit-energy leaping in a chain**
  through the air from his raised mace out across the frame, and **two or three translucent ANCESTOR-SPIRIT
  rat silhouettes** standing solemnly at his back, mirroring his stance.

  ancestor ghosts, war-paint, headdress, mace, standing solemn.** **Zamorei = the TIDE — a towering WAVE,
  sea-mail, salt and kelp, staff, braced low.** Never swap their signatures.
- Also distinct from Shockaa (Elemental — lightning from the sky) and Twihnz (Enhancement — dual-wield melee).
- Per STYLE.md healers read **tough** — Fazcafe is a grizzled war-painted veteran, never a gentle mystic.
