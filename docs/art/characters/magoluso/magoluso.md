# Magoluso — "The Arcane Architect"

- **Class / spec:** **Arcane Mage — DPS** (MAIN)
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 11 Jul 2026 · profile-banner sweep (one banner per raider)
- **Toon name:** **Magoluso** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** sleek dark-grey fur, prominent whiskers, cold calculating eyes lit **arcane violet-blue**,
  clawed hands. Lean, precise, arrogant — a scholar-duelist who has burned through armies. Hard, never soft.
- **Class read:** **Arcane Mage** — **violet-blue arcane geometry**. His magic is *built*, not thrown:
  floating runes, rotating sigil-rings, mathematical constructs. Cold, exact, overwhelming.
- **Armor:** a battle-scholar's dark coat-robe — deep charcoal with **violet-blue rune-embroidery** running
  the seams, a high stiff collar, iron-plated shoulders and forearm bracers over the cloth, a torn
  scholar's sash. War-worn, singed at the hem.
- **Weapon:** ONE **arcane staff** — dark iron latched around a floating, slowly rotating violet crystal
  that does not touch the shaft — held in ONE clawed hand. **Other hand is open, building the spell.**
  NO second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** **rotating arcane sigil-rings** — two or three concentric violet-blue rune-circles
  hanging and turning in the air around his free hand, the spell assembling itself inside them.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** charcoal coat, iron grey, **arcane violet-blue** glow, cold white rune-light, deep shadow.
  Class colour Mage `#3FC7EB` (the strip tints toward that blue).
- **Key light:** the **rotating sigil-rings** at his hand — a cold, hard violet-blue light thrown up onto
  his face and collar from the side; deep dark elsewhere.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

- **Character card (21:9):** staff at rest, rings turning around his raised hand, ruins reflected in the light.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text):** **the construct** — standing calm and still in a 3/4 turn, one hand
  held out flat and level to the side with **concentric arcane rune-rings rotating around it**, the spell
  building; staff angled in the other hand; his cold eyes on the viewer, unhurried. He sits RIGHT, dark
  left third. (Deliberately the only *calm, controlled, unmoving caster* in the set — his power is precision.)
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** cold violet-lit muzzle, high collar, a rune-ring edge in frame.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** lower-center inside a vast arcane sigil, rings towering above him.
- **Combat / lore moment:** an arcane barrage assembling above advancing dark silhouettes.

## Notes / history

- Distinct from **Yakiroo** (the guild's other mage): **Yakiroo = FIRE — orange-gold pyroblast, chaotic heat.**
  **Magoluso = ARCANE — violet-blue geometry, cold, controlled, sigil-rings.** Never give Magoluso fire.
