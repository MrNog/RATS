# Khaddash — "The Whisper in the Dark"

- **Class / spec:** **Shadow Priest — DPS** (MAIN)
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 11 Jul 2026 · profile-banner sweep (one banner per raider)
- **Toon name:** **Khaddash** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).
- **⭐ ELITE TIER — #7 DPS guild-wide (avg ~5,505).** Big, dramatic, mind-shattering composition.

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** ash-black fur, prominent whiskers, eyes drowned in **shadow-purple** light that leaks out as thin
  wisps, clawed hands, gaunt and hollow-cheeked — a rat who has stared into the void too long. Grim, unnerving.
- **Class read:** **Shadow Priest** — mind-flay, madness, whispers. **Shadow-purple/violet-black** power.
  His flavour is **the SCREAM** — psychic force, not fire.
- **Armor:** heavy dark priest's vestments over a scarred iron cuirass — blackened robe layered with rune-stitched
  cloth, a torn shoulder-mantle, dangling censers and prayer-chains gone black, a deep hood shadowing the face.
- **Weapon:** ONE **shadow staff** — dark bone-and-iron topped with a caged, screaming void-skull — gripped
  in ONE clawed hand. His **other hand is out, mind-flaying**. NO second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** **twin beams of shadow-purple mind-flay** lancing from his outstretched clawed hand,
  and a ring of **shadow-wisp faces / whispering skulls** orbiting his head — the voices.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** ash-black, violet-black robe, cold iron, **shadow-purple** psychic glow, sickly grey-white.
  Class colour Priest `#E6E6E6` (UI only — the render glow is SHADOW-PURPLE).
- **Key light:** the **shadow-purple mind-flay** from his own hand and staff — an unnatural, dark-violet
  under-light that carves his hollow face out of the black; almost no ambient.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

- **Character card (21:9):** hooded, staff planted, wisp-faces circling, shadow pouring from his palm.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text · EPIC):** **the mind-flay** — turned 3/4, one clawed hand thrust FORWARD
  and low toward the left, twin lances of shadow-purple mind-flay tearing out of his palm across the strip;
  the staff's void-skull screaming in the other hand; whispering wisp-faces spiralling around his hood.
  He sits RIGHT, dark left third.

- **Combat / lore moment:** flaying a line of dark silhouettes with beams of psychic shadow.
