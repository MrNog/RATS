# Radnah — "The Beast Warden"

- **Class / spec:** **Hunter — DPS** (MAIN)
- **Rank:** Raider Rat

- **Toon name:** **Radnah** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** rust-brown fur, prominent whiskers, warm fierce eyes lit **hard amber**, clawed hands, an old
  claw-scar raked across the chest. Broad-shouldered, rugged, loud — a beastmaster who fights *with* his pack.
- **Class read:** **Hunter — the BEAST side.** His companion is the star: he doesn't aim, he **commands**.
- **Armor:** heavy fur-and-mail — a shaggy beast-pelt mantle over dark scaled mail, bone trophies and fangs
  strung across the chest, hide-wrapped forearms, a heavy tusked helm slung at his belt (not worn).
- **Weapon:** ONE **heavy recurve bow** — held in ONE clawed hand, DOWN and away, **not drawn** (he isn't
  shooting; he's directing). NO second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** **his BEAST** — a huge scarred **war-bear / dire-beast** mid-lunge beside him, and
  **his free arm flung out**, pointing, commanding it forward. Master and beast, one motion.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** rust-brown, shaggy beast-pelt, dark scaled mail, bone-white trophies, **hard amber** glow,
  earthy hunter-green ambient. Class colour Hunter `#AAD372` (the strip tints to that green).
- **Key light:** a warm low **amber-gold** firelight from the front-left; deep forest-dark behind.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

- **Character card (21:9):** standing with the beast at heel, bow slung, trophies swinging.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text):** **companion command** — his free arm flung out and FORWARD to the
  LEFT, pointing/roaring an order; the bow held down in the other hand; and his **huge war-bear LUNGING
  past him** into the frame, jaws open, all muscle. He sits RIGHT (slightly behind the beast). Dark left
  third. (The only banner where a *beast* shares the frame as a co-subject.)
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** rugged scarred muzzle, amber eyes, a fang-trophy at the collar, the
  beast's shoulder just behind him.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** lower-center with the beast, a dark pine forest rising above.
- **Combat / lore moment:** loosing the beast at a line of advancing dark silhouettes.

## Notes / history
