- **Class / spec:** Discipline Priest (MAIN)

## Locked look (the fixed identity)

- **Rat:** dark fur, prominent whiskers, sharp fierce eyes, clawed hands — a grim, hardened veteran, a
  battle-priest, NOT delicate, soft or angelic.
- **Class read:** Discipline Priest **Starcaller** — a caster of **light AND shadow** (Disc's duality),
  themed cosmic/starlit — starlight and void woven together.
- **Weapon:** ONE ornate **staff** in one hand (a caster's staff, head crowned with a starlit focus).
- **Signature detail:** **light-and-shadow duality** — pale gold-white holy light on one side, deep
  shadow-violet/void on the other, with **starlight motes / a small constellation sigil** he conjures
  (the "Starcaller" theme). Disc's translucent shield-bubble of light optional.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** battle-worn dark charcoal robes with deep-violet and bronze-gold trim, hard-edged shoulder
  pieces; **pale gold-white holy light** + **deep shadow-violet/indigo void**, cosmic starlight accents.
  Grim and war-worn, NOT flowing pastel. Priest class colour for text/UI is `#E6E6E6` (near-white).
- **Key light:** dual source — **pale gold-white holy light** and **shadow-violet void** cast from his
  staff/hands, meeting on him; cold cosmic ambient behind so both read against the dark.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

- **Character card (21:9):** grounded caster's stance, staff raised, light on one hand + shadow on the
  other, a constellation sigil forming above · ambient = dark starlit cathedral ruin / cosmic void.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text):** bust/action crop pushed RIGHT, staff angled, one hand light one hand
  shadow, starlight motes, dark low-detail left third for text overlay.
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** grim head, half-lit gold / half shadow-violet, starlit eyes.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** full figure lower-center, staff aloft splitting light/shadow, a starfield
  rising above; dark top + bottom for clock/home-bar.
- **Combat / lore moment:** raising the staff to weave light and void on a defiant stand, starlight surging,
  dark silhouettes at the edges (content-safety).
