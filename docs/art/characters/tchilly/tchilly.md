# Tchilly — "The Plague Tongue"

- **Class / spec:** **Shadow Priest — DPS** (MAIN) · offheals when needed
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 11 Jul 2026 · profile-banner sweep (one banner per raider)
- **Toon name:** **Tchilly** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** dark grey-brown fur, prominent whiskers, cold eyes lit **pale violet-white**, clawed hands —
  lean, stooped, patient. A preacher of rot. Grim and unsettling, NOT cute.
- **Class read:** **Shadow Priest** — the **WORD** flavour. Where Khaddash flays and Coizinho reaps,
  Tchilly's power is **spoken**: shadow-word curses that crawl out of his mouth and eat the world.
- **Armor:** a heavy plague-doctor's dark vestment — layered black-and-ash robes over a battered iron
  breastplate, a **long beaked iron plague-mask pushed UP onto his brow** (so his muzzle and eyes stay
  readable), heavy bandage-wraps, a **swinging iron censer** chained at his hip trailing violet smoke.
- **Weapon:** ONE **short iron sceptre/mace** — a stubby black rod capped with a violet-lit reliquary —
  in ONE clawed hand. His **other hand is raised open, palm out, mid-incantation**. NO second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** the **shadow-word** — jagged **violet rune-glyphs pouring OUT OF HIS OPEN MOUTH**
  and spiralling forward through the air, and creeping **violet rot-veins** spreading across the ground
  wherever the words land.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** ash-black robe, iron grey, bandage-white, **violet-white** shadow-word glow, sickly
  grey-violet rot. Class colour Priest `#E6E6E6` (UI only — the render glow is VIOLET-WHITE).
- **Key light:** the **violet glyphs streaming from his mouth**, lighting his face from BELOW/FRONT — an
  eerie, unnatural up-light on the muzzle and the raised beak-mask; deep dark elsewhere.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

- **Character card (21:9):** stooped forward mid-sermon, censer swinging, rot spreading at his feet.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text):** **the spoken curse** — leaning FORWARD and low, mouth OPEN mid-word,
  a torrent of jagged violet rune-glyphs pouring out and streaming LEFT across the strip; free hand raised
  palm-out; the censer swinging and trailing violet smoke; rot-veins creeping along the ground beneath.
  He sits RIGHT, dark left third. (The only "magic from the mouth" banner in the set.)
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** beaked mask pushed up on the brow, violet-lit muzzle, glyphs at the lips.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** stooped lower-center in a plague-ward, glyphs spiralling up the frame.
- **Combat / lore moment:** speaking a curse across a field of advancing dark silhouettes.

## Notes / history

- **The three shadow priests are deliberately split — never blur them:**
  - **Khaddash** = the MIND-FLAY: hooded, twin beams from an outstretched hand, whispering wisp-faces.
  - **Coizinho** = the REAPER: bare head, iron eye-plate, two-handed shadow-SCYTHE, back-turned wind-up.
  - **Tchilly** = the PLAGUE-TONGUE: beaked mask on the brow, censer, glyphs pouring from his MOUTH.
