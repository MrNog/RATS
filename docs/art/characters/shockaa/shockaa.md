# Shockaa — "The Stormcaller"

- **Class / spec:** **Elemental Shaman — DPS** (MAIN)
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 11 Jul 2026 · profile-banner sweep (one banner per raider)
- **Toon name:** **Shockaa** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).
- **⭐ ELITE TIER — #5 DPS guild-wide (avg ~5,560).** Give him a big, dramatic, storm-scale composition.

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** storm-grey fur standing on end with static, prominent whiskers, fierce eyes crackling
  **electric blue-white**, clawed hands wreathed in arcing current. Lean, hard, weather-beaten.
- **Class read:** **Elemental Shaman** — raw **lightning**. Blue-white current, storm-mail, totems.
- **Armor:** heavy weathered storm-mail — dark blue-grey scaled plate hung with bone fetishes, feathers and
  beaded totem-charms, a ragged storm-blue cloak snapping in the wind, spirit-masked pauldrons.
- **Weapon:** ONE **storm hammer/mace** — a heavy iron mace crackling with blue-white current — in ONE
  clawed hand. His **other hand is open and calling the lightning**. NO second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** **a bolt of lightning striking DOWN into his raised hand** from the storm overhead,
  and a **stone totem** planted at his feet arcing with the same current.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** storm-grey, dark blue-grey mail, bone fetishes, **electric blue-white** lightning, wet slate.
  Class colour Shaman `#0070DD` (the strip tints to that blue — perfect match).
- **Key light:** the **lightning strike itself** — a violent, hard, blue-white flash from ABOVE, blowing out
  the shadows for an instant; deep storm-dark everywhere else.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

- **Character card (21:9):** mace low, hand raised, totem beside him, storm gathering.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text · EPIC):** **the strike** — one arm thrust straight UP, a jagged bolt of
  lightning slamming DOWN out of the storm INTO his open palm, current forking off his mace, mail and
  whiskers; the flash lights him hard from above. He sits RIGHT, dark left third.
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** static-lifted fur, blue-white eye-crackle, storm-mail collar.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** lower-center on a cliff, a colossal thunderhead filling the frame above.
- **Combat / lore moment:** calling a chain of lightning across advancing dark silhouettes.

## Notes / history

- **The three shamans are deliberately split:** **Shockaa = ELEMENTAL — lightning FROM THE SKY, mace, totem,
  hard flash from above.** Twihnz = Enhancement (dual-wield melee). Fazcafe/Zamorei = Resto (healing water).
  Never blur them.
