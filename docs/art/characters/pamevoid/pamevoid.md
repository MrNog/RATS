## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** black fur shot through with veins of creeping **void-purple**, prominent whiskers, eyes burning
  as **empty violet voids** (no pupils — twin holes of light), clawed hands tipped in shadow. Tall, gaunt,
  imperious, utterly war-worn. Terrifying, NOT cute.
- **Class read:** **Demonology Warlock** — the **VOID/shadow** flavour, not the fel-green one. His power is
  **void-purple and abyssal black**, and his signature is his **demon**.
- **Armor:** heavy dark warlock's plate-and-robe — blackened iron pauldrons carved into demonic faces over a
  ragged void-purple robe, chains and hanging sigil-charms, a tattered high collar and torn cloak that
  frays into shadow at the hem (the cloth literally dissolving into void).
- **Weapon:** ONE **warlock staff** — black iron twisted around a caged void-crystal — held in ONE clawed
  hand. His **other hand is open and casting**. NO second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** **his DEMON** — a colossal void-purple **felguard/void-demon silhouette** looming
  up BEHIND him, twice his height, half-dissolved into shadow, its eyes the same violet voids as his.
  Master and demon, one silhouette. **The demon is the epic beat — do not omit it.**

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** abyssal black, void-purple, deep violet, cold iron, a rim of bruised magenta.
  Class colour Warlock `#8788EE` (the strip tints to that violet — perfect match).
- **Key light:** the **void-purple energy he is unleashing**, hard and frontal, plus the demon's violet
  eye-glow burning from behind him — he is lit from BOTH sides by his own power.
