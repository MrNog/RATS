# Mongoloide — "The Judgement"

- **Class / spec:** **Retribution Paladin — DPS** (MAIN) · também joga **Protection** (mesmo rato, spec tank)
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 15 Sep 2026 · ICC intake sweep (promoted up through the ICC tier)
- **Toon name:** **Mongoloide** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).

> Paladins are separated by **character**, not spec. **Okanor** = Holy, amber rune-seams, the Light forged
> into a war-mace. **Solanarrage** = Holy, the blinding "dark sun" crusader-executioner. **Rellik** = Ret,
> the enormous rune-etched greatsword wreathed in consecration fire.
> **Mongoloide** = Ret, but the **HAMMER** read: judgement delivered downward. Heavy, blunt, final —
> where Rellik sweeps, Mongoloide drops. **Never give Mongoloide a sword** — that is Rellik's silhouette.

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** coarse **grey-brown** fur, matted, lighter à volta do focinho e da testa; bigodes brancos longos,
  orelhas grandes e redondas com o interior rosa-baço. Olhos **âmbar-dourados** fundos e semicerrados sob
  uma sobrancelha carrancuda, focinho largo e rombo, e uma **cicatriz vertical vermelha fina a descer a
  testa entre os olhos**. Broad, thick-set through the shoulders and utterly still — an executioner's
  stillness, not a duellist's.
- **Class read:** **Retribution Paladin — the HAMMER of judgement.** His Light is a **downward vertical**:
  a column of hard gold-white falling from above onto the ground in front of him, or the hammer already
  driven into the stone. Weight, not speed.
- **Armor:** heavy, square, battle-scarred **dark steel plate** com bordas grossas de **bronze fosco
  oxidado e rebites redondos** — pauldrons angulares em laje, peitoral sulcado, colarinho de malha ao
  pescoço, e um **tabardo de pano vermelho-sangue escuro** até ao joelho. Gauntlets far too large. Worn
  like a uniform, not a trophy. Bronze gasto de campo de batalha, **nunca ouro polido**.
- **Weapon(s):** ONE **enormous two-handed WAR-HAMMER** — a squared blackened-steel head on a long haft,
  gold-inlaid on the striking face. Construction, spelled out because models get it wrong: at the BOTTOM a
  plain butt-cap, then a long HAFT, then the heavy squared HEAD at the TOP. **BOTH clawed hands grip the
  haft, stacked, in the LOWER half — never up at the head.** The whole hammer reads as one solid object,
  fully visible, never clipping through his body or vanishing behind his head. NO sword, NO shield, NO
  second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** **the falling column of judgement** — a hard vertical shaft of gold-white light
  coming straight DOWN out of frame onto the ground just ahead of him, and the hammer either raised
  overhead in both hands at the top of the swing, or already **planted head-down in cracked stone** with
  both hands stacked on the butt of the haft. Vertical always.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette — TRAVADA em "bronze fosco" (escolha dele, 21 Sep 2026):** pelo cinzento-acastanhado, aço
  escuro arranhado com **bronze fosco oxidado**, tabardo vermelho-sangue, olhos âmbar. **Ele não gosta de
  dourado ornamentado ("à la Elden Ring")** — bronze gasto e mate, nunca gilding polido nem filigrana
  brilhante. Class colour Paladin `#F58CBA` — nunca na arte, só no texto/UI.
- **Key light:** the vertical column — a hard **smoky amber-bronze** top-down key striking his pauldrons,
  brow and the hammer head, with deep shadow beneath everything. Cool grey fill behind.

## Protection (spec tank — mesmo rato)

Mesmo focinho, mesma cicatriz, mesmo plate. Muda só o armamento: **martelo de UMA mão** numa mão +
**escudo torre plantado** na outra. A luz continua vertical e fria, **sem chama** — é isso que o separa do
[`gathlock`](../gathlock/gathlock.md), que é espada + escudo a arder em fogo dourado.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

Locked = the look above. Pose, camera, scene change every image (STYLE.md's ONE rule).

- **Character card (21:9):** standing dead square to camera, feet planted wide, the war-hammer **planted
  head-down** in cracked flagstone before him with both hands stacked on the butt of the haft, chin level,
  the column of light falling on him from directly above · ambient = a ruined cathedral floor open to a
  black sky, broken pillars, a cracked stone dais.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text):** upper body pushed RIGHT, three-quarter turned LEFT into the frame, the
  hammer head raised into the top-right corner clear of his skull, gold-white light falling down his near
  side · ambient = dark broken stone and black sky falling to flat black on the left.
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** stern head-and-shoulders, brow-scar and muzzle lit hard from directly
  above, the hammer haft crossing one corner. Deep shadow under the brow.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** full standing figure low-center, hammer planted, the column of light rising
  the full height of the frame above him into a dark sky; keep the top 20% dark for the clock.
- **Combat / lore moment:** at the top of an overhead swing, both hands on the haft, hammer raised high, the
  light column landing on the ground ahead of a line of dark armored silhouettes — the instant BEFORE, never
  the impact. No bodies.
