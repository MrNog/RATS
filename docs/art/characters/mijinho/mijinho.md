# MIJINHO — "The Diseaseful Shaman"

- **Class / spec:** Shaman — "Degradation" (flavor spec: a corrupted plague-caster; render with Elemental/
  Resto shaman cues — totems, storm-blue lightning — soured into sickly plague-green).
- **Rank:** rat
- **Commissioned:** 28 Jul 2026 · by Okanor

## Locked look (never changes between images)

- **Rat:** filthy sewer-grey and sickly-brown mottled fur, matted and clumped, prominent whiskers, clawed
  hands with long cracked yellow nails. **Crazed wide bulging eyes** with tiny pinprick pupils and a
  mad unblinking stare — the signature. Patches of raw discoloured hide showing through thin fur
  (blotchy, weathered, mangy — no wounds, no gore). Lean, wiry, hard-muscled and sinewy — a starved,
  ropey plague-hermit build, never chubby or bulky. Exactly ONE long scaly tail.
- **Armor:** essentially none — he abandoned it. A **filthy tattered grey rag loincloth / strip of ruined
  cloth wrap** at the waist, frayed and stained, over a bare scarred torso. Optionally a few crude
  bone-and-twine fetishes and small clay vials strung across the chest on a cord. Bare clawed feet.
  He is a wretched hermit, not an armored raider — the *absence* of armor is the point.
- **Weapon(s):** **ONE** item only — a **big sealed clay-and-glass jar of glowing plague-green sewer brew**,
  gripped in one hand by a rope handle, thick sludge sloshing inside, a faint green glow lighting his face
  from below. The other hand is free for casting. NO extra weapons, no staff, no totem in hand.
- **Signature detail:** the **glowing green jar** + the **crazed bulging eyes**, and a thin trail of
  luminous plague-green vapour curling from his bared teeth as he grins.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** sewer-grey and sickly-brown fur, filthy oatmeal-grey rags, **toxic plague-green** glow
  (dominant accent), muted storm-blue shaman lightning as the secondary accent, wet dark-stone
  browns and charcoal.
- **Key light:** the **jar itself** — sickly green underlight from below on his face and chest, with a
  cold storm-blue rim light from behind/above (the shaman's storm) separating him from a very dark
  desaturated background.
