- **Class / spec:** **Feral Combat Druid — cat DPS / bear offtank** (MAIN) · *confirmed from log rows*

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** heavy dark-brown fur with a broad scarred back and a torn ear, prominent whiskers, fierce eyes lit
  hard **amber**, thick clawed hands — **big, broad and heavy** (a bear-shifter's mass) yet still lean and
  muscular, never fat. Battle-scarred and immovable.
- **Class read:** **Feral Druid — the BEAR side.** Where the guild's other feral is quick and cat-like,
  Alturas reads as **the warden who absorbs the hit**: heavy, planted, half-shifted toward bear.
- **Armor:** thick battered hide-and-bone armor — layered dark pelt, a mantle of shaggy fur across huge
  shoulders, bone plates lashed over the chest and forearms, everything scored with claw-gouges.
- **Weapon:** **his CLAWS** — massive bear-claws, no held weapon. NO staff, NO sword.
- **Signature detail:** a **partial BEAR shift** — hulking shoulders, a broadened jaw with bared fangs, and a
  smouldering **amber-gold** feral aura burning off his back and forearms.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** dark-brown fur, shaggy black pelt, bone-white plates, **amber-gold** feral glow, iron-grey.
  Class colour Druid `#FF7C0A` (the strip tints toward that warm orange — which suits his amber aura).
- **Key light:** a hard **amber-gold** feral glow from his own aura, low and frontal; cold shadow behind.
