# Solanarrage — "The Dark Sun"

- **Class / spec:** **Holy Paladin — healer** (MAIN)
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 11 Jul 2026 · profile-banner sweep (one banner per raider)
- **Toon name:** **Solanarrage** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).
- **⭐ ELITE TIER — #6 HPS guild-wide (avg ~2,300).** One of the guild's top healers — give him scale.

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** dark iron-grey fur, prominent whiskers, hard unforgiving eyes lit **harsh gold**, clawed
  gauntlets, an old burn-scar up one side of the jaw. Tall, heavy, stern. **A DARK-KNIGHT WHO WIELDS THE
  LIGHT** — per STYLE.md: battered heavy plate, grim face, the holy gold as *harsh radiant power*, NEVER
  a soft angelic look.
- **Class read:** **Holy Paladin** — but read him as a **crusader-executioner who happens to heal**. The
  Light is a weapon he turns on his own wounds. Harsh, blinding, punishing gold.
- **Armor:** massive battle-scarred **blackened plate** with tarnished gold filigree — deep-gouged pauldrons
  crowned with sun-motifs, a heavy dented breastplate bearing a **cracked sun-sigil**, a torn crimson-and-gold
  tabard, bare grim head (no helm).
- **Weapon:** ONE **heavy two-handed war-hammer / maul** — dark iron, gold-banded — held in **BOTH hands**
  and **RAISED HIGH**. NO shield, NO second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** the **DARK SUN** — a colossal, harsh **golden sun-disc / halo-ring of holy light**
  burning in the air BEHIND and ABOVE him, throwing him into near-silhouette. The light is *blinding and
  punishing*, not warm and gentle.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** blackened plate, tarnished gold, deep crimson tabard, cold iron, **harsh blinding gold**.
  Class colour Paladin `#F58CBA` (UI only — the render glow is **HARSH GOLD**, never pink).
- **Key light:** the **golden sun-disc BEHIND him** — a hard, blinding backlight that rims his armor in
  fire-gold and drops his front into deep shadow. Dramatic contre-jour.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

- **Character card (21:9):** hammer grounded, sun-disc behind, gold burning through the gaps in his plate.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text · EPIC):** **the backlit crusader** — hammer hauled HIGH overhead in BOTH
  hands, arms locked, the colossal golden sun-disc blazing BEHIND him so he reads as a **near-black
  silhouette rimmed in molten gold**, holy light lancing out from behind his shoulders in hard rays.
  Low camera. He sits RIGHT, dark left third. (The only true *contre-jour / backlit silhouette* in the set.)
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** burn-scarred grim jaw, harsh gold eyes, gold rim burning off the pauldron.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** lower-center, the sun-disc filling the whole upper frame.
- **Combat / lore moment:** a hammer raised at the head of a shattered line, holy light breaking the dark.

## Notes / history

- **Per STYLE.md, healers read TOUGH.** Solanarrage is explicitly a **dark-knight-with-the-Light**: blackened
  plate, harsh gold, punishing. **NEVER** soft, angelic, white-winged, haloed-and-serene, or pink.
- Distinct from Gathlock and Okanor (the other paladins) — Solanarrage owns the **giant sun-disc backlight**.
