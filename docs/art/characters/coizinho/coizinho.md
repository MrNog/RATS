# Coizinho — "The Void Reaper"

- **Class / spec:** **Shadow Priest — DPS** (MAIN) · offheals when needed
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 11 Jul 2026 · profile-banner sweep (one banner per raider)
- **Toon name:** **Coizinho** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** pale bone-grey fur, prominent whiskers, one eye burnt out and covered by a **dark iron eye-plate**,
  the remaining eye burning **cold violet**, clawed hands. Wiry, sharp-featured, quietly vicious.
- **Class read:** **Shadow Priest** — but the **REAPER / dark-devotion** flavour. Where Khaddash flays the
  mind with beams, Coizinho reads as a **shadow-priest executioner**: shadow gathered into a blade of force.
- **Armor:** a heavy **shroud-cassock** — long blackened priest's robe cinched with an iron war-belt over a
  scarred half-cuirass, sleeves torn to the elbow, a hanging stole of black prayer-cloth covered in violet
  sigils. **No hood** (deliberately — Khaddash owns the hood).
- **Weapon:** ONE **long shadow-scythe/reaper's staff** — a curved dark blade on a black haft — gripped in
  **BOTH hands**, swept low behind him. NO second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** the **shadow blade** — the scythe's edge is a burning violet-black crescent of
  gathered void, trailing a wide arc of shadow through the air behind his swing.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** bone-grey fur, blackened robe, cold iron, **violet-black** void edge, dead-white sigils.
  Class colour Priest `#E6E6E6` (UI only — the render glow is VIOLET-BLACK).
- **Key light:** the **violet crescent of the scythe blade**, low and BEHIND him, back-lighting his
  silhouette; a hard cold rim; his face lit from the side by the one violet eye.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

- **Character card (21:9):** scythe grounded, robe hanging still, one violet eye on the viewer.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text):** **the wind-up / backlit reaper** — turned nearly BACK-TO-CAMERA with
  his head twisted around over his shoulder to stare LEFT out of the frame, the scythe swept far back and
  low in both hands, its violet crescent burning BEHIND him and rim-lighting his whole silhouette. He sits
  RIGHT, dark left third. (The only back-turned, over-the-shoulder-stare banner in the set.)
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** the iron eye-plate, one burning violet eye, torn stole at the collar.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** standing lower-center in a black ossuary, the scythe arc lighting the vault.
- **Combat / lore moment:** a wide shadow-arc sweeping toward advancing dark silhouettes (no contact shown).

## Notes / history

- **The three shadow priests are deliberately split — never blur them:**
  - **Khaddash** = the MIND-FLAY: hooded, twin beams from an outstretched hand, whispering wisp-faces.
  - **Coizinho** = the REAPER: no hood, iron eye-plate, a two-handed shadow-SCYTHE, back-turned wind-up.
  - **Tchilly** = the PLAGUE-TONGUE: see his sheet (mask, censer, creeping shadow-word rot).
