# Someday — "The Long Fuse"

- **Class / spec:** **Fire Mage — DPS** (MAIN)
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 15 Sep 2026 · ICC intake sweep (joined during the ICC tier)
- **Toon name:** **Someday** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).

> Three mages, separated by **character**, not spec. **Magoluso** = arcane geometry, violet-blue, magic that
> is *built*. **Jiskob** = controlled fire, held in the hands, never leaves his grip. **Someday** = the
> opposite of Jiskob: **fire already LET GO** — thrown, airborne, out of his hands and on its way. If Jiskob
> is the held ember, Someday is the moment after the release.

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** dusty red-brown fur bleached pale at the tips as if scorched, prominent whiskers, half-lidded
  tired eyes lit a dull **coal-red**, clawed hands. A long white scar down one cheek. Tall and loose-limbed,
  shoulders dropped — he throws fire like a man who has done it ten thousand times and is not impressed.
- **Class read:** **Fire Mage — the RELEASED half.** His signature is always a flame *in the air, leaving* —
  a thrown bolt mid-flight, a fading trail behind an open hand. His hands are empty; the fire has gone.
- **Armor:** a long weather-beaten travelling coat-robe over dark mail — dust-red and faded, hem burnt
  ragged, one sleeve shorter than the other, a heavy rolled scarf at the throat, black-and-gold Horde trim
  half worn away. Practical, lived-in, unglamorous.
- **Weapon(s):** ONE **short wand or rod** shoved through his belt and NOT held — both hands stay free.
  NO staff, NO second weapon, NO book. If a weapon must read, it is the belt-rod, never in a hand.
- **Signature detail:** **one open throwing hand** with a fading fire-trail curling off the fingers, and
  the **cast flame already airborne and leaving frame** — a clean streaking bolt of coal-red fire, well
  clear of his body. Empty hands, fire in flight.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** dust-red fur, faded travelling-coat red-brown, dark mail, burnt hem, one hot **coal-red**
  streak. Class colour Mage `#3FC7EB` — keep a **cold blue-cyan ambient** behind so the strip tints correctly
  against his warm fire.
- **Key light:** the departing bolt — a hot **coal-red** rim raking one side of his face and the throwing
  arm, already falling off as the fire leaves; cold blue-grey fill on the other side.
