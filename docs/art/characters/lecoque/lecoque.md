# Lecoque — "The Wrath of the Grove"

- **Class / spec:** **Balance Druid — Boomkin DPS** (MAIN)
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 11 Jul 2026 · profile-banner sweep (one banner per raider)
- **Toon name:** **Lecoque** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).
- **⭐ ELITE TIER — #9 DPS guild-wide (avg ~5,350).** Push this banner harder than a standard one: bigger
  spell, more drama, a real cataclysmic moment.

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** dark bark-brown fur, prominent whiskers, sharp fierce eyes burning **astral violet-white**,
  clawed hands — lean, wiry, hard-eyed. A druid who **fights**, NOT a soft nature-spirit.
- **Class read:** **Balance Druid** — the wrath of the grove. His signature is the **lunar/astral** half of
  Balance: **violet-white starfire and moonlight**, with emerald nature only as a secondary accent.
- **Armor:** battered dark leather-and-bark harness with bone and antler fittings, a mantle of **black raven
  feathers** across the shoulders, frayed moss-green wrappings stained and war-worn.
- **Weapon:** ONE **gnarled druid staff** — dark wood grown around a raw moonstone at the crown — gripped in
  **BOTH hands** for the channel. NO second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** **antlers** rising from his brow, a **partial moonkin cast** (feathers flaring at the
  arms), and a **violet-white starfire vortex / rune-circle** he is calling down from above.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** bark-brown, black raven-feather, moss-green leather, **violet-white astral** starfire,
  cold silver-white moonlight, a touch of emerald. Class colour Druid `#FF7C0A` (UI only — render glow is
  VIOLET-WHITE ASTRAL).
- **Key light:** the **astral starfire beam falling from above**, blasting down onto him — a hard, dramatic
  top-light; cold moonlight rim behind.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

- **Character card (21:9):** braced wide, staff planted, starfire spiralling up the shaft.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text · EPIC):** **two-hand channel calling down a starfall** — staff gripped in
  BOTH hands and thrust UP, a huge violet-white astral rune-circle burning in the sky, a COLUMN of starfire
  crashing DOWN through the frame onto him, feathers and cloak blasted upward by the force. Low camera,
  he sits RIGHT, dark left third. (The only "column of light from the sky" banner in the set.)
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** antlered head, violet-white eye-glow, feathers at the shoulder.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** lower-center under a colossal moon, starfire raining down the frame.
- **Combat / lore moment:** calling a starfall onto an advancing line of dark silhouettes.

## Notes / history

- **Spec confirmed from the roster screenshot (11 Jul 2026): Balance.** An earlier draft made him Feral —
  he and Alturas were exactly swapped. Corrected: **Lecoque = Boomkin, Alturas = Feral.**
- **The two druid DPS are deliberately split:** Lecoque = **astral VIOLET-WHITE starfire, staff, antlers,
  calling down the sky.** Alturas = **amber-gold bear, claws, no weapon, roaring.** Never blur them.
- Distinct from the resto druids (Liyondry/Onetreeheals) — Lecoque is pure destruction, never a healing glow.
