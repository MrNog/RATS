# Setanegra — "The Black Arrow"

- **Class / spec:** **Hunter — DPS** (MAIN)
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 11 Jul 2026 · profile-banner sweep (one banner per raider)
- **Toon name:** **Setanegra** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).

> Hunter spec doesn't drive the art (user: *"hunter doesn't matter spec"*) — the three hunters are separated
> by **character**. Setanegra = **the dark stalker** (his name reads "black arrow" — lean into it).

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** jet-black fur, prominent whiskers, cold pale eyes like **green-white slits**, clawed hands —
  gaunt, silent, low to the ground. A night-hunter, more shadow than rat. Grim, never cute.
- **Class read:** **Hunter — the STALKER / trapper side.** Not a proud archer and not a beast-roarer: a
  poacher of the dark. Traps, snares, poison, one arrow from somewhere you never looked.
- **Armor:** black hunting leathers — a hooded oilskin over dark studded hide, a scarf/half-mask pulled up
  over his muzzle (**pull it DOWN below the muzzle so his face still reads**), snare-wire and trap-hooks
  hung across the chest, a **black-fletched quiver** low on the hip.
- **Weapon:** ONE **short black recurve bow**, held **low and horizontal** in one clawed hand, an arrow
  pinched ready in the other — **nocked but NOT yet drawn**, held down at waist height, the hunter's
  low-carry. NO second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** a **black raptor / dark-feathered hunting bird** perched tense on his shoulder or
  a low branch beside him, and a faint **poison-green shine** on the arrowhead.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** jet-black leathers, dark moss-green oilskin, gunmetal, black fletching, a thin **poison-green**
  arrow shine. Class colour Hunter `#AAD372` (the strip tints to that green — matches the poison glow).
- **Key light:** a **cold pale moon-rim from behind**, edging his hood and shoulders out of near-black;
  a faint sickly green up-shine from the arrowhead onto his muzzle. Very dark overall — an ambush.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

- **Character card (21:9):** crouched over a set snare, raptor watching, bow across the knees.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text):** **the crouched stalk** — down LOW in a hunting crouch, half-hidden
  behind a broken branch/rock in the foreground, bow held low and horizontal, arrow pinched ready but
  UNDRAWN, head lifted and eyes locked LEFT out of the frame; the black raptor tense above him. He sits
  RIGHT and LOW. Dark left third. (The only crouched, low-to-the-ground, half-concealed banner in the set.)
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** hooded, scarf at the throat, pale green-white slit eyes, raptor feather.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** crouched lower-center on a ruined wall, a black moonlit swamp above.
- **Combat / lore moment:** loosing one silent arrow from cover at distant dark silhouettes.

## Notes / history

- **The three hunters are deliberately split — never blur them:**
  - **Radnah** = the BEAST-MASTER: bow **down**, arm flung out commanding, a **war-bear lunging**. Warm amber.
  - **Kinven** = the MARKSMAN: **full draw**, string to the cheek, a wolf, cold dawn ridge, standing tall.
  - **Setanegra** = the DARK STALKER: **crouched low, half-concealed**, bow undrawn at the waist, a **black
    raptor**, poison-green shine, near-black night. Never give him a full draw or a bear.
