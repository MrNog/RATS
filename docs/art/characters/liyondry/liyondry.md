## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** deep umber-brown fur going grey at the muzzle, prominent whiskers, hard weathered eyes lit
  **jade-green**, clawed hands — an old, scarred, iron-willed keeper. **Hardened, never delicate.**
- **Class read:** **Resto Druid** — a grim warden of life, not a gentle nature-priest. Jade-green healing
  light is harsh and rooted, like a tree splitting stone.
- **Armor:** heavy dark hide-and-bark vestments over battered leather, wrapped in **living root and creeping
  vine** that has grown through the armor itself, a heavy hooded mantle of dark moss and stitched pelts.
- **Weapon:** ONE **root-grown druid mace/idol-staff** — dark heartwood knotted around a raw jade stone —
  gripped in ONE clawed hand. NO second weapon.
- **Signature detail:** **living roots and vines erupting from the ground around his feet and coiling up his
  arm**, jade rejuvenation-runes glowing where they touch him.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** umber-brown, dark bark, deep moss-green, **jade-green** healing glow, wet stone grey.
  Class colour Druid `#FF7C0A` (UI only — the render glow is JADE-GREEN).
- **Key light:** **jade-green light rising from the ground/roots** (a hard uplight, harsh and radiant, NOT
  a soft angelic halo); cold dark ambient above and behind.
