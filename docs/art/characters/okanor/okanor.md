# Okanor — "The Lightkeeper"

- **Class / spec:** Holy Paladin (frontline healer) (MAIN)
- **Rank:** Warchief
- **Commissioned:** founding officer
- **Alts:** Rogue (Okanata) · Blood DK (Okanath) · Elemental Shaman (Okanstorm) · Feral Druid (Okanthorn) —
  see the **Alts** section at the bottom.

## Locked look (never changes between images)

- **Rat:** dark fur, prominent whiskers, sharp fierce eyes, clawed hands — a **stern, hardened** frontline
  healer: a dark-knight who wields the Light, NOT a soft angelic saint. Battle-worn, grim resolve.
- **Armor:** heavy battle-worn **dark plate** — charcoal / blackened iron with **bronze-gold trim** and
  **burning amber runes** that glow like forge-fire along the seams; scuffed and dented from the front line.
  Dark and grim — **NOT ivory / white / pale**.
- **Weapon(s):** a **large one-handed WAR-MACE** in the RIGHT hand — a **long haft** topped with a heavy
  **angular flanged head** (bladed/spiked flanges, ornate gold-and-iron), imposing; **NOT a small round
  ball mace**. + a **Horde-crested shield** (amber rune-glow on the crest) on the LEFT forearm. One item
  per hand — never stacked. NO extra weapons.
- **Signature detail:** the **burning amber rune-glow** running the seams of his dark plate + the hot
  flanged mace — the Light forged into a war-mace, not a soft angelic aura. Render CLEAN (radiant
  edge-light, NOT floating ember particles or speckled dots).

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** charcoal / blackened-iron plate, bronze-gold trim, burning amber `#C8A96E`-to-orange runes,
  deep shadow. Warm and forge-lit — harsh radiant power, dark and manly (no pastels, no pink, no soft angelic wash).
- **Key light:** warm **forge-fire amber** from the rune-glow + hot mace as the dominant source; cold shadow
  behind so the dark plate and amber runes read hot against the black.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

What's **locked** is the LOOK above (dark plate, amber rune-seams, flanged war-mace, Horde shield, stern
face). **Everything else rotates** — pose, camera, and especially the **SETTING**. Do NOT default to the
forge/cathedral; it's just one option. **Vary the environment every time:** snowy mountain pass · Icecrown /
ICC frozen citadel · ToC sand-gold arena · Ulduar titan-bronze halls · a warm interior (Horde war-tent,
firelit hall, chapel) · a ridge at dusk · a wintry courtyard. Keep the **amber rune-glow on him** so the
locked look survives whatever the scene's ambient light is.

- **Character card (21:9):** pose = grounded protective healer's stance, flanged war-mace low and ready in
  the right hand, Horde shield forward on the left — a healer who holds the line, not a floating caster ·
  ambient = ash-dark forge-hall / ruined chapel, cold stone falling to black, amber rune-glow the only warmth.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text):** upper-body 3/4 pushed to the RIGHT, dark low-detail left third for text
  overlay · dominant **gold/amber** class-glow · ambient = shadowed forge-stone, rune-lit dark architecture behind.
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** stern head-and-shoulders, three-quarter gaze, amber rune-light rimming
  the face, edge of the shield visible; single warm rim light on a dark backdrop.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** full standing figure lower-center, mace + shield; a towering dark
  forge/cathedral rising above; keep top ~20% and bottom darker for clock/home-bar.
- **Desktop wallpaper (16:9, text-free):** full standing figure slightly LEFT of center, mace + shield,
  dark forge-cathedral filling the right as negative space · DONE — this is the approved look (see history).
- **Combat / lore moment:** planted between the raid and the dark, shield raised, mace hot with amber
  rune-light — a defiant protective stand, never slaying (content-safety rules).
