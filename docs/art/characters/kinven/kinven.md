# Kinven — "The Steady Shot"

- **Class / spec:** Hunter — ranged physical (MAIN)
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 11 Jul 2026 · profile-banner sweep (one banner per raider)
- **Toon name:** **Kinven** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).
- **Note:** the guild also has a **Kinvein** (Death Knight). Different toon — do not confuse.

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** dusty brown-grey fur, prominent whiskers, one eye narrowed in a permanent sighting squint, sharp
  eyes lit hard **amber**, clawed hands — wiry, weathered, still as stone. A marksman, patient and cold.
- **Class read:** **Hunter/Marksman** — the bow is everything. Earthy brown-green kit, no magic glow beyond
  the arrow's amber shine.
- **Armor:** worn dark ranger's mail-and-leather — a scarred hauberk under a hooded oilskin cloak, a heavy
  quiver of black-fletched arrows at his back, straps and buckles everywhere.
- **Weapon:** ONE **great recurve bow**, held in one clawed hand, drawn with the other. NO second weapon,
  no sword, no melee weapon on show.
- **Signature detail:** the **full draw** — string to the cheek, a single arrow burning faint **amber** at
  the head; and a **battle-scarred wolf companion** hunched low beside him.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** earthy brown, moss-green oilskin, dark iron mail, black fletching, **hard amber** arrow glow.
  Class colour Hunter `#AAD372` (the strip tints toward that green).
- **Key light:** cold dawn light raking in low from the LEFT (the direction he is aiming), catching the bow
  and his sighting eye; deep shadow behind him.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

- **Character card (21:9):** standing full-draw, wolf circling at his feet.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text):** **full draw, seen from BEHIND-AND-SIDE** — camera set behind his drawing
  shoulder, bow arm extended LEFT across the strip, string to the cheek, sighting down the arrow toward the
  dark left third; the wolf crouched at his flank. He sits RIGHT. (The only over-the-shoulder-behind camera
  in the set.)
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** the sighting squint, hood up, amber eye, bowstring cutting the frame.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** kneeling on a ridge lower-center, a vast cold valley above.
- **Combat / lore moment:** loosing into a line of dark silhouettes cresting a ridge.
