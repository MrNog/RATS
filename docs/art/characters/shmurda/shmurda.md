## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

## ⚠️ AI-pose rule (learned the hard way)

**Never put the weapon above, behind or across his head.** Every overhead / wind-up / raised-blade generation
came back broken: the head fused into the shoulder, the arms bent the wrong way, the hands mangled, the sword
floating unattached. Image models cannot resolve two hands *and* a face when both are stacked behind the skull.

Keep it safe: **hands at chest height or lower, always in clear frame, always separated from the head by
open space.** Prefer sword point-down on the pommel, sword carried low at the side, or sword resting on a
shoulder *pauldron* with the hands still low. His power reads through mass, stance and scale — not a raised blade.
