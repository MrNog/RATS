# Twihnz — "Twin Storms"

- **Class / spec:** **Enhancement Shaman — melee DPS** (MAIN)
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 11 Jul 2026 · profile-banner sweep (one banner per raider)
- **Toon name:** **Twihnz** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).
- **⭐ ELITE TIER — #8 DPS guild-wide (avg ~5,375).** Big, kinetic, high-energy composition.

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** dark slate fur with a white lightning-streak scar down one side of the face, prominent whiskers,
  wild fierce eyes lit **blue-white**, clawed hands. Compact, coiled, explosive — a brawler.
- **Class read:** **Enhancement Shaman** — a **melee** shaman. **DUAL-WIELD** is his entire signature: two
  weapons, always, one in each hand, both enchanted with storm.
- **Armor:** battered storm-mail — dark scaled plate over hide, wolf-pelt mantle, bone and feather fetishes
  braided into the fur, wrappings on the forearms, a short ragged storm-blue kilt-cloak.
- **Weapon:** **TWO one-handed storm axes/maces** — ONE IN EACH clawed hand, exactly two, never stacked in
  one hand. **Windfury lightning arcs off BOTH heads.** NO staff, NO shield, NO third weapon.
- **Signature detail:** the **dual-wield cross** — both weapons swept out wide to the sides with forking
  blue-white current trailing each edge, and **spirit-wolf silhouettes** running low at his flanks.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** dark slate, storm-blue mail, wolf-pelt grey, bone-white, **blue-white** windfury current.
  Class colour Shaman `#0070DD` (the strip tints to that blue).
- **Key light:** the **blue-white current forking off both weapons**, hard and low from the front — lit by
  his own storm; deep dark behind.

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

- **Character card (21:9):** mid-stride, both axes out, wolves at his heels.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text · EPIC):** **the dual-wield cross, mid-charge** — caught MID-STRIDE
  charging LEFT, torso torqued, BOTH axes swept out wide to the sides with lightning forking off each
  edge, spirit-wolves streaking low beside him. Motion-blur in the cloak. He sits RIGHT, dark left third.
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** the lightning-scar face, wild blue-white eyes, an axe-head in frame.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** charging lower-center, a storm-torn sky above.
- **Combat / lore moment:** a wolf-flanked charge into dark silhouettes, both axes crackling.

## Notes / history

- **The three shamans are deliberately split:** **Twihnz = ENHANCEMENT — MELEE, DUAL-WIELD (two weapons!),
  charging, spirit-wolves.** Shockaa = Elemental (lightning from the sky, one mace, static). Fazcafe/Zamorei
  = Resto (healing water). If Twihnz ever renders with a staff or a single weapon, it is wrong.
