## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** mottled grey-and-green fur, prominent whiskers, narrow calculating eyes lit a faint **toxic
  green**, clawed hands. A leather patch strapped over one ear, chewed short. Compact, crouched, always
  lower to the ground than everyone else in the frame.
- **Class read:** **Survival Hunter — the TRAP side.** He is never mid-shot. He is always *setting*
  something, or watching something he already set.
- **Armor:** practical field gear — dark mottled hide and oiled canvas, a heavy tool-harness across the
  chest strung with **coiled wire, iron trap-jaws, stoppered vials and fuse-cord**, mud-caked greaves,
  black-and-gold Horde trim worn almost off. Gloves cut at the fingers.
- **Weapon(s):** ONE **short heavy crossbow**, held in ONE hand, LOWERED and pointed at the ground — never
  raised, never aimed, never drawn. NO second weapon, NO bow, NO companion beast.
- **Signature detail:** **an armed iron trap** — jaws open and faintly lit toxic-green — set on the ground
  at his feet or held in his free hand, and **his free hand doing something**: driving a stake, paying out
  wire, thumbing a fuse. He is always *rigging*, never firing.
