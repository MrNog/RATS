# Unfrostblood — "The Green Winter"

- **Class / spec:** **Unholy Death Knight — DPS** (ALT of [`grokara`](grokara.md))
- **Rank:** Raider Rat (alt)
- **Commissioned:** 21 Sep 2026 · pedido junto com a correção das armas do Grokara
- **Toon name:** **Unfrostblood** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased; lives in
  the main's folder as `images/profile-bg/grokara/unfrostblood.png`).

> Four Death Knights on the roster, separated by **character**, not spec:
> **Cryptwall** = Blood tank, crimson runes, ONE two-handed greatsword held crosswise — the wall.
> **Mojo** = Unholy plague-knight, TWO dripping green axes — rot and disease.
> **Nutelaa** = Frost, TWO frost-blue axes — cold and crystalline.
> **Unfrostblood** = Unholy too, but the **name is a contradiction and so is he**: a **MISMATCHED pair**,
> frost-blue sword in one hand and a green-runed axe in the other. That odd pairing is his whole silhouette
> — a matching pair of axes is Mojo's and Nutelaa's, the two-hander-held-crosswise is Cryptwall's.

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** dark slate-grey fur going near-black at the crown, prominent whiskers, clawed hands. **Eyes lit
  hard necrotic green** — the undead glow, the one warm-cold thing in the face. Lean and rangy, standing
  slightly hunched, like something that does not need to breathe.
- **Class read:** **Unholy DK** — decay and the risen dead, but **cold**. Not Mojo's dripping plague: this
  is a **frozen** unholy, green fire over blue ice, a winter that rots.
- **Armor:** heavy dark **blue-grey saronite plate**, scarred and pitted, with a gold-trimmed shoulder
  clasp. **Big jagged spiked pauldrons** — the left noticeably heavier than the right, spikes curving
  upward. Tattered cloth hanging at the hip in **toxic yellow-green**, and deep rust-brown leather boots
  and belts. Nothing polished; everything grave-cold.
- **Weapon(s):** **DUAL-WIELD — a MISMATCHED PAIR, one weapon per hand.** In one clawed hand ONE **curved
  one-handed RUNEBLADE**: a broad single-edged sword in **frost-blue steel**, gold-wrapped hilt, a pale
  rune-line down the blade. In the OTHER clawed hand ONE **one-handed AXE**, darker and heavier, its head
  etched with runes lit **necrotic green**. Exactly two weapons, never stacked in the same hand, never a
  third. **NOT two matching axes** (that is Mojo and Nutelaa), **NO two-hander** (Cryptwall), NO shield.
- **Signature detail:** **the mismatched pair itself** — cold blue sword in one hand, green-runed axe in the
  other, and **necrotic-green fire boiling up around his feet and licking off the axe head**. Blue steel,
  green fire: the contradiction in his name, made visible.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** dark blue-grey saronite, near-black fur, **necrotic green** `#00FF96`-ish fire, **frost-blue**
  blade, toxic yellow-green cloth, rust-brown leather, weathered gold trim. Cold and grave-lit. Class colour
  Death Knight `#C41E3A` — red only in the page/UI, **never** in the render.
- **Key light:** the **green rune-fire off the axe head and the ground** is the dominant source, lighting the
  underside of the jaw and the pauldron spikes; the runeblade carries its own thin cold blue rim on the other
  side. Everything else falls to deep black.
