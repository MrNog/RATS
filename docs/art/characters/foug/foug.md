## Locked look (never changes between images)

- **Rat:** dark fur, prominent whiskers, sharp fierce eyes glowing **cold necrotic green**, clawed hands — a
  rugged, weathered, battle-hardened veteran. Keeps his **Scottish** grit — grim, seasoned, unshakeable; a
  raider who's seen everything. NOT cute or soft.
- **Armor:** heavy battle-worn **dark DK plate** — blackened iron with a faint **bone-grey** cast, corroded
  edges, cold **necrotic-green rune-glow** running the seams and the blade; spiked pauldrons, a tattered dark
  cloak. Grim and dark, NOT ivory/white/pale.
- **Weapon(s):** **DUAL-WIELD — TWO one-handed runeswords, ONE in each hand**, each blade etched with runes
  glowing cold necrotic green. Exactly two swords, one per hand — never stacked, never a third weapon, no
  shield. (Two matching swords, NOT one big two-hander.)
- **Signature detail:** his **risen ghoul / undead minion** lurking at his side (Unholy's pet — replaces the
  old mist-beast companion), + the cold **green rune-glow** on his plate and the twin blades.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** blackened iron, bone-grey, cold necrotic green, deep shadow. Dark and cold. (DK class colour
  for text/UI is `#C41E3A`, but the render **glow is green**, not red.)
- **Key light:** cold **necrotic-green** rune/blade glow as the dominant source; cold blue-grey ambient
  behind so the green reads against the dark. Keep his green a clean **rune-glow**, not dripping plague —
  he's a veteran knight, not a disease-carrier (see Notes).

## Per-format pose + ambient (SUGGESTIONS, not locks — vary every time)

What's **locked** is the LOOK above (dark DK plate, green rune-seams, TWIN one-handed runeswords, risen
ghoul, grim veteran face). **Everything else rotates** — pose, camera, and especially the **SETTING**. Vary the
environment every time: Icecrown / ICC frozen citadel · Ebon Hold necropolis · a snow-blasted Northrend
ridge · Ulduar titan-halls · a dark battlefield at dusk · Acherus interior. Keep the **green rune-glow on
him** so the locked look survives whatever the scene's ambient light is.

- **Character card (21:9):** grounded veteran's stance, twin runeswords held low/crossed or one back one
  forward, ghoul crouched at his side · ambient = dark frozen battlefield / necropolis, cold and grim.
- **Profile banner (4:1, no text):** bust pushed RIGHT, one sword raised across the body (second at the
  side), cold green eye-glow, dark low-detail left third for text overlay · ambient = shadowed Icecrown ramparts.
- **Discord icon (1:1, face crop):** grim head + spiked pauldron, cold green eye + rune-glow rimming the face.
- **iPhone wallpaper (9:19.5):** full standing figure lower-center, twin runeswords in hand, ghoul below, a
  towering necropolis / ICC spire above; dark top + bottom for clock/home-bar.
- **Combat / lore moment:** twin runeswords raised to command the risen dead, green energy surging — a
  defiant advance into dark silhouettes, no gore (content-safety).
