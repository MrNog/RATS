# Jiskob — "The Kindling"

- **Class / spec:** **Fire Mage — DPS** (MAIN)
- **Rank:** Raider Rat
- **Commissioned:** 15 Sep 2026 · ICC intake sweep (joined during the ICC tier)
- **Toon name:** **Jiskob** (profile banners are keyed by this exact toon name, lowercased).

> Three mages on the roster, separated by **character**, not spec. **Magoluso** = arcane geometry, violet-blue,
> magic that is *built*. **Jiskob** = **controlled fire** — a forge-smith's fire, held and shaped in the hands.
> **Someday** = wild fire, thrown. Jiskob's read is DISCIPLINE: the fire never leaves his grip.

## Locked look (the fixed identity — everything else is free to reinvent)

- **Rat:** dark soot-black fur with burnt-copper patches at the muzzle and ears, prominent whiskers, eyes
  lit a steady **ember-orange**, clawed hands. Old burn-scarring across both forearms — self-inflicted, the
  mark of someone who handles his own fire. Lean, still, controlled.
- **Class read:** **Fire Mage — the CONTROLLED half.** His flame is compressed and deliberate: a tight
  burning sphere held in one palm, heat-shimmer off the knuckles. Never a wild blast, never an explosion.
- **Armor:** a smith's take on battle-robes — heavy charcoal layered cloth over a scorched leather apron and
  bracers, soot-blackened at the hems, black-and-gold Horde trim at the collar. Sleeves rolled to the elbow
  so the burn-scars show.
- **Weapon(s):** ONE **iron-shod staff**, gripped in ONE hand, planted or angled across the body — its head
  a plain blackened iron cage holding a **single caged ember** that lights his face from below. NO second
  weapon, NO orb, NO book.
- **Signature detail:** **the caged ember** in the staff head, plus a **tight sphere of fire held in the free
  open palm** — steady and contained, never erupting. He holds fire the way a smith holds hot iron.

## Palette & key light

- **Palette:** soot-black fur, charcoal cloth, scorched leather, blackened iron, burnt-copper, one hot
  **ember-orange** core. Class colour Mage `#3FC7EB` — the profile strip tints cyan even though his fire is
