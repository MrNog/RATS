# ⚒️ Dwarf wallpapers — anime cut

Wallpapers with **dwarves** (real dwarves, NOT rats, NOT humans). They keep the RATS rules that make the art
work (grim/masculine tone, anatomy & gear correctness, clean render, content-safety, vary every scene) and
swap the painterly WoW render for an **anime key-visual** look.

Inspiration: the two cinematic shots (13 Oct 2026) — huge braided red beards with iron rings, a shaved
head with a red mohawk and a blue rune tattoo, bare scarred arms, fur-and-leather pauldrons, a stone-headed
warhammer, a dark stone hall lit by one torch.

## Style block (already inside every prompt below)

```
Anime key visual, high-budget dark fantasy anime film look (Ufotable / MAPPA / Berserk-film feel): bold clean line art, cel-shaded skin, fur and armor with two-tone hard shadows, richly painted cinematic backgrounds, dramatic rim light, deep black shadows, World of Warcraft dwarf design. Grim, masculine, battle-hardened. Adult rugged faces, heavy brows, broken noses, weathered skin.
```

**Dwarf body:** short and **stocky-muscular** — broad shoulders, thick forearms, barrel chest that is
muscle not gut, short powerful legs, big hands. This replaces the rats' "lean" rule. Never fat, never
chibi. Every dwarf gets his own face and beard (mohawk / bald / braided crown / grey-streaked / burned).

**RATS cut (optional, per prompt):** black-and-gold battered plate, blood-red cloth, brush-stroke title in
`#C8A96E`, small `RATS` watermark bottom-right. Without it the wallpaper is fully text-free. No Alliance or
Horde insignia either way: these dwarves carry clan runes only.

## Do NOT include (already inside every prompt below)

```
Do NOT include: rats, rat features, tails, human-height bodies, elves, extra or duplicated limbs/heads/weapons, weapon and shield in the same hand, malformed hands or wrong finger count, over-rotated or broken joints, floating or over-tilted head, chibi or cartoon proportions, big shiny moe eyes, pretty-boy or young smooth faces, pastel or pink colours, fat belly, floating dust or ember speckle, heavy haze, blown-out bloom, manga panels, sound-effect lettering, Alliance or Horde symbols, modern clothing, gore or blood, plain white background. [No-text version: also any lettering, title, watermark or logo.]
```
