# Okanor's Forge — site icon (1:1, NO text)

The browser-tab icon of mrnog.github.io/Okanor-s-Forge, also the phone home-screen icon. Shown from
**16×16 px** (tab) to 180×180 (apple-touch-icon).

**Idea: the forge mark.** Okanor's flanged mace resting across a black anvil, struck metal glowing orange.
No rat face: at 16 px a face turns to mud, while an anvil + mace silhouette still reads.

- **Square 1:1**, export 1024×1024. Scale down to `images/icons/favicon.png` (32×32 and 64×64 look best)
  and `images/icons/apple-touch-icon.png` (180×180).
- **One bold silhouette**, centered, small margin. The dark backdrop is part of the icon.
- **NO text, letters or numbers.**

```
Digital fantasy illustration, square 1:1 (1024x1024), World of Warcraft game icon style, painterly, dark-souls Horde mood, bold and simple like a WoW spell icon.

Subject: a heavy black iron blacksmith's anvil seen from a low 3/4 angle, centered. Light & palette: one hot orange key light from the glowing metal, catching the anvil's edge and the mace flanges; deep shadow elsewhere; charcoal, black iron, bronze-gold, forge orange. Very high contrast so the shape reads when shrunk to 16x16 pixels.

Render quality: CLEAN and crisp — NO sparks, NO embers, NO particles, NO haze, NO bloom. Sharp edges on the anvil horn, the flanges and the haft.

Do NOT include: any lettering, number, logo, rune text or watermark; a rat or any character; a round ball-headed mace, a hammer, a sword, a second weapon; tongs, a forge fire scene, a workshop or table; busy background detail; a border, frame or rounded corners; pastel or pink colours; plain white background.
```
