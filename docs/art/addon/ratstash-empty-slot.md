The picture inside every **empty slot** of the RatStash bag and bank windows, and in the "Free" slot.
Shown at about **37×37 px**, dozens at once, next to real item icons.

- **Square 1:1**, export 1024×1024. Becomes a 64×64 (or 128×128) BLP DXT5 in `RatStash/Media/`.
- Must read as **empty** at a glance: darker and quieter than any item icon, one faint motif in the middle.
- Fills the whole square edge to edge (the game draws its own slot frame on top), no border of its own.
- **NO text, logo or letters.**
