# Raid banners: Ruby Sanctum (10 / 25, Normal / Heroic)

**STYLE.md Format F** (Raid-Helper header, 16:9, ~1728×960, carries text). Four banners, one per sign-up:

| File | Subtitle | Hero | Camera | Light |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `images/banners/RS 10.png` | `10 MAN` | one lone rat | ground level, straight on | crimson fire |
| `images/banners/RS 25.png` | `25 MAN` | a column of many rats | low angle up the stairs | crimson fire |
| `images/banners/RS 10 HC.png` | `10 HEROIC` | 2–4 rats | over-the-shoulder at the Twilight rift | violet twilight beats fire |
| `images/banners/RS 25 HC.png` | `25 HEROIC` | the whole raid, tiny | high bird's-eye, straight down | half fire, half twilight |

**Normal vs Heroic reads at a glance:** Normal is the red dragonflight's sanctum burning (warm crimson and
molten orange). Heroic is the Twilight taking over (cold violet and indigo, with fire losing). 10 vs 25 reads
by scale: a few rats against a procession or a whole raid.

**Lockouts (3.3.5a):** 10 and 25 are separate lockouts. Normal and Heroic of the **same size share one
lockout**, so the guild runs 10 N *or* 10 HC in a given week, not both (same for 25). Heroic is the raid
difficulty setting, not a separate instance. Separate Raid-Helper events still make sense because a
Heroic night and a Normal night ask for different people.

**What makes it the Ruby Sanctum:** pale cream stone terraces carved with coiling dragon reliefs,
**crimson-leaved trees**, raw **ruby crystals**, under Wyrmrest Temple. Halion's platform is a raised stone
ring ringed by burning trees. The Twilight realm is the same place as a cold violet shadow-copy. Keep it
from reading as a volcano or a lava lake.

Title `RUBY SANCTUM` in sandy tan-gold `#C8A96E` (blood-red is the Fangs tier only). The top third stays
dark for the title. Bottom-right stays dark for the RATS watermark.

> **Logo tip:** the generator *recreates* the RATS logo from text. Generate first, then paste the real
> `RATS` PNG bottom-right in an editor.
