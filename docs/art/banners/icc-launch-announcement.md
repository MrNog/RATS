# Discord announcement — ICC phase launch (5 September)

Copy-paste text for the launch post + the two Raid-Helper sign-ups.

The launch post is written in the **Loremaster voice** (same as `chronicles/`) — real WoW lore anchors, no
spell names, Horde guild voice, rat/cheese flavor, Discord markdown, under 2000 characters. It is a
**call-to-arms on the eve**, not a victory retelling and not a memo: the gates open on the 5th, the swarm has
not gone in yet.

It also **closes the loop the ToC chronicle opened**. That one ended with the Argent Crusade asking who is
worthy to march on the Lich King. This is the answer, and it says so.

The practical details (times, what to bring) sit in the Raid-Helper blocks where people actually look for
them — the launch post carries the lore, one line of plan, and the link. A wall of bullet points buried in
the middle is what killed the first draft.

**Fill in before posting:** `#CHANNEL` (the sign-up channel), the unix timestamp for the 5th, and the
Raid-Helper links once the events exist. Times are **20:00 server**.

The 5th is an **availability check**, not a locked roster — the guild opens on 10-man to learn the fights,
and the 25 goes up once we know who's around.

## Notes

### Lore anchors (all real WotLK canon)
- **Tirion Fordring** built the Argent Coliseum on the ice of Icecrown to test champions for the assault on
  the citadel — the ToC chronicle is the direct setup for this post.
- **Arthas Menethil**, the Lich King, sits the **Frozen Throne** at the citadel's summit.
- **Kel'Thuzad** served as Arthas' lieutenant and returned as a lich; **Lord Marrowgar** is the citadel's
  first guardian; the **Blood-Queen Lana'thel** rules the Crimson Hall; the **val'kyr** serve the Lich King.
- **Naxxramas** and **Ulduar** are the guild's own prior clears, per the ToC chronicle.
- No spell names, per guild voice.

### Post mechanics
- Discord dynamic timestamps (`<t:unix:D>`) render the date in each reader's own timezone. Generate the unix
  value for 5 September, 20:00 server time before posting — the placeholder is deliberate, since server time
  is not the same as yours.
- The **25-man is mandatory** and the **10-man is optional** under the guild's attendance rules. The launch
  post stays out of it; the Raid-Helper blocks carry it.
- If the 25 gets a firm date after sign-ups close, edit the Raid-Helper event rather than posting a new one,
  so existing sign-ups carry over.
- Post is under 2000 characters including markdown — check again if you edit it, Discord hard-cuts there.
