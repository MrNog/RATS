# Promo art: Ruby Sanctum launch (7 November)

The **announcement image** for the last raid of Wrath, the image that sits at the top of the Discord post.
It is **not** a raid banner, so it has no `10 MAN` / `25 MAN` subtitle and no dates baked in.

**This is a lore piece, not a roster shot.** The subject is Halion and the sanctum split between two realms.
The rats are small, anonymous silhouettes, there to give the scene its scale. No faces, no named officers.

## Getting the Ruby Sanctum to look like the Ruby Sanctum

"Dragon + fire + lava" describes half of Warcraft, and the generic AI banner (dragon head on the left,
ornate emblem in the middle) is what that produces. Name the sanctum by its **specific** features:

- **A sacred grove, not a volcano.** The red dragonflight's sanctum is a hall of pale stone terraces and
  **crimson-leaved trees** growing among huge **ruby crystals**, under Wyrmrest Temple. Fire is invading
  the place. Fire is not what the place is made of.
- **Wyrmrest above.** The pale white temple tower of the Dragonblight is visible high above through a
  break in the rock. That one landmark places the image in WotLK.
- **Two realms, one fight.** Halion is fought in the physical realm and the Twilight realm at the same
  time. That is the image: the frame is **split down the middle**, with burning crimson on the left and
  cold violet twilight on the right. Halion straddles the seam, half fire, half shadow.
- **The war-band splits too.** The tiny rat silhouettes divide into two groups, one walking into each realm.
  That is the raid's own mechanic, and it matches the "every rat counts" line in the post.

Title `RUBY SANCTUM`, tagline `THE TWILIGHT DESTROYER AWAITS`, with no date so the image survives a schedule
change. Sandy tan-gold `#C8A96E`. Blood-red is reserved for the Fangs tier, even though this raid is red.

**Layout:** the top ~third is open dark sky for the title. Halion is the central mass on the seam, the two
rat groups sit small in the lower third, one on each side, and the bottom-right stays shadowed for the RATS
watermark.

> **Logo tip:** the generator *recreates* the RATS logo from text, not your PNG. Generate first, then paste
> the real `RATS` PNG bottom-right in an editor.

## Variants worth a reroll

- **No text at all:** strip the "Visual identity" paragraph and add "NO lettering, title, name, watermark
  or logo" to the Do-NOT clause. This gives a clean plate to lay the announcement over.
- **No rats:** cut the Subjects paragraph. Just Halion on the seam, the purest lore shot.
- **From Wyrmrest down:** camera at the top of Wyrmrest Temple looking DOWN through the broken floor into
  the burning sanctum, with Halion's split silhouette far below. Trades the dragon's size for vertigo.
- **The three lieutenants:** Baltharus, Saviana and Zarithrian as dark silhouettes on terraces leading up
  to Halion. More lore, but busier. Only try it if the main version comes out too empty.
