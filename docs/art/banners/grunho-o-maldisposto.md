# Grunho, o Maldisposto — parody movie poster

A joke piece: the "grumpy supervillain + army of yellow henchmen" movie poster, starring Grunho
([sheet](../characters/grunho/grunho.md)) and the rats as his goggled henchmen. **Deliberate humour exception**
to the grim tone — the comedy comes from battle-hardened warrior rats taking the silly uniform dead seriously.

> **IP tip:** don't name the film or "Minions" in the prompt (ChatGPT may refuse or water it down) — the
> look is described instead. The title is our own pun, so it's fine.

