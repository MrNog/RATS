# Raid banner — Trial of the Grand Crusader, 25 Heroic

**STYLE.md Format F** (raid banner, 16:9, ~1728×960, carries text), but the framing is deliberately
**broken away from the other four ToC banners**.

The existing four are all the same shot: one rat, back to camera, centered, walking into the arena at
ground level, warm sand-and-gold light. This one is the opposite on every axis:

| | The four existing banners | This one |
|---|---|---|
| Title | `TRIAL OF THE CRUSADER` | `TRIAL OF THE GRAND CRUSADER` — the real heroic-mode name |
| Subtitle | `10 MAN` / `25 MAN` | `25 HEROIC` |
| Camera | ground level, eye height | **high above, looking straight DOWN** into the arena |
| Subject | one rat, back to camera, big-ish | **the whole raid**, tiny, a ring of figures |
| Floor | intact wet sand | **shattered open** — Anub'arak's fissure swallows the center |
| Light | warm gold from the far gate | **cold Icecrown blue rising from the pit**; braziers lose |

Title stays the standard raid-banner sandy tan-gold `#C8A96E` (blood-red is the Fangs tier, not this).
Anub'arak bursting up through the coliseum floor is the canonical end of the instance — see
[`../chronicles/trial-of-the-crusader.md`](../chronicles/trial-of-the-crusader.md) — so the pit is
lore-true, not just mood.

**Layout:** the top ~third stays dark storm-sky for the title; the arena ring sits center-low; the black
fissure is the dead-center focal point. Nothing occupies the bottom-right but shadow, so the RATS
watermark has room.

> **Logo tip:** the generator will *recreate* the RATS logo from text, not copy your PNG. Generate first,
> then paste the real `RATS` PNG bottom-right in an editor if you want it pixel-perfect.

## Variants worth a reroll

- **Tighter crop** — push the camera lower and closer so the fissure fills half the frame and only the
  near arc of stands is visible. More claustrophobic, less map-like.
- **Show more of him** — let the chitinous plates and a second eye-glow read more clearly in the pit if
  the first pass buries him entirely. Still no full creature, no gore.
- **Ground-level fallback** — if the bird's-eye keeps coming out flat or map-like, the same beat works at
  a worm's-eye angle: camera on the sand, stands looming over, the crack running toward the lens.
