# Promo art — ICC phase launch (5 September)

The **announcement image** for the new phase opening — the piece that sits at the top of the Discord post
telling the guild ICC is live and sign-ups are open. It is **not** a raid banner: no `10 MAN` / `25 MAN`
subtitle, no sign-up details baked in. Those change; the art shouldn't.

**This is a lore piece, not a roster shot.** The subject is the citadel and the Frozen Throne above it. The
rats are present but tiny and anonymous — scale and a hint of who's coming, nothing more. No readable
armor, no named officers, no faces. If a viewer's eye lands on a rat before it lands on the spire, the
image failed.

## Getting Icecrown to look like Icecrown

The first pass of this prompt read as a coliseum, because "colossal fortress, spires, skull motifs, glowing
runes" describes half of Warcraft. ICC has to be named by its **specific** silhouette or the generator
defaults to a generic arena gate:

- **A mountain-fused fortress, not a free-standing building** — ICC is carved INTO and grown OUT OF the
  Icecrown glacier. Black saronite and ice merging with the mountain, not a structure sitting on flat ground.
- **Vertical, never circular** — a tapering tower stack climbing to a single point. The moment the frame
  suggests a ring, an oval, or tiered seating, it reads as ToC. Say so explicitly in the negatives.
- **The Frozen Throne on top** — the dais at the summit above everything, tiny and cold-lit. That is ICC's
  one unmistakable feature and the reason the shot works from a distance.
- **Skeletal, spiked, asymmetric** — flying buttresses like ribs, jagged spikes, bone-white against black
  saronite. ToC is warm wood-and-iron symmetry; ICC is cold black bone.
- **Suspended in the sky** — the upper citadel hangs above a sheer glacier cliff face, gulfs of open air
  under it. Nothing about a coliseum floats.

Title reads `ICECROWN CITADEL` with the subtitle `THE GATES OPEN` — a date-free tagline, so the same image
survives if the schedule moves. Sandy tan-gold `#C8A96E`; blood-red is the Fangs tier only.

**Layout:** the top ~third is storm sky for the title + tagline; the citadel silhouette is the center focal
mass; the war-band is a small dark smear on the low ridge; bottom-right stays shadow for the RATS watermark.

> **Logo tip:** the generator *recreates* the RATS logo from text, not your PNG. Generate first, then paste
> the real `RATS` PNG bottom-right in an editor for a pixel-perfect mark.

## Variants worth a reroll

- **No rats at all** — cut the Subjects paragraph entirely. Pure architecture and wasteland. The most
  lore-forward version of this shot, and the safest if the figures keep drawing focus.
- **No text at all** — strip the "Visual identity" line and add "NO lettering, title, name, watermark or
  logo" to the Do-NOT clause. Gives a clean plate to lay the announcement text over in Discord or on the hub.
- **The Lich King's shadow** — a vast dark crowned silhouette suggested in the storm clouds above the
  summit, never a clear face. Ominous, but risks the generator drawing a literal character.
- **From the causeway** — camera on one of the narrow ice bridges leading to the upper citadel, gulfs of air
  either side, the throne above. Keeps the vertical read, trades the wasteland scale for vertigo.
