# Raid banner — Onyxia + Trial of the Crusader (combined 25 night)

**STYLE.md Format F** (raid banner, 16:9, ~1728×960, carries text), but a **split diptych**: one banner for a
single raid night that clears **BOTH** raids back-to-back. The frame is divided down the middle — **Onyxia's
Lair** (hot, red, volcanic) on the LEFT, the **Trial of the Crusader** coliseum (cold, sand-and-gold arena) on
the RIGHT — joined by a seam of light/smoke that a small rat vanguard walks straight into.

This deliberately breaks the "one instance = one banner" default. It's the same locked Format-F look (back-to-
camera rats, small in frame, the instance is the star, gold `#C8A96E` brush title top-center, RATS watermark
bottom-right) — just **two instances sharing the shot**, split hot vs cold so both read instantly.

| | Standard Format F | This one |
|---|---|---|
| Instances | one | **two** — Onyxia (left) + ToC (right) |
| Title | one raid name | **two stacked lines**: `ONYXIA'S LAIR` / `TRIAL OF THE CRUSADER` |
| Subtitle | one size | `25 MAN` centered under both (one shared night) |
| Frame | one entrance flanks both sides | **split**: fiery lair left, cold arena right, bright seam center |
| Light | one key light | **two** — red/orange lair glow left, pale sand-gold arena glow right, meeting at the seam |
| Hero | one rat | **a tight vanguard of 3 rats** walking the seam, back to camera |

Title stays the standard sandy tan-gold `#C8A96E` (blood-red is the Fangs tier, not this).

**Layout:** top ~third stays dark for the two-line title + subtitle; the vanguard sits center-low on the seam;
bottom-right stays shadowed for the RATS watermark. The seam of light is the vertical centerline and focal axis.

> **Logo tip:** the generator *recreates* the RATS logo from text, not your PNG. Generate first, then paste the
> real `RATS` PNG bottom-right in an editor for a pixel-perfect mark.
