`ICC 10.png` is the reference shot for Format F: ground level, one lone rat, back to camera, centered,
walking in between the two skull gate-arches. This 25 keeps every locked token (gold `#C8A96E` brush title

| | `ICC 10.png` | This one |
|---|---|---|

| Camera | eye level, straight on | **low angle** looking up the causeway, gates towering over |

banners in the sign-up channel should know which night is which before reading the subtitle.

**Layout:** the top ~third stays dark storm-sky and shadowed spire for the title + subtitle; the column sits
center-low on the causeway; bottom-right stays shadow for the RATS watermark.

## Variants worth a reroll

- **Wider column** — if fifteen-plus rats come out as a mush, drop to eight to ten clearly separated figures
  in two staggered ranks. The read (a war-band, not a scout) matters more than the literal count.
- **Gates opening** — the two doors mid-swing, the blue light spilling out in a widening wedge across the ice
  as the column steps into it. More cinematic, harder to keep the title area dark.
- **From inside** — camera behind the gates looking OUT, the column arriving as silhouettes against the storm.
  Reverses the light (bright outside, dark frame) — a strong reroll if the standard shot feels too close to
  the 10-man.
