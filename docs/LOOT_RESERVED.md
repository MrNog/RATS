# RATS — Reserved Loot (Council-Only)

**Status:** draft for officer review · **Phase:** ICC (P4) · **Last updated:** 2026-09-17

This list exists to answer one question *before* an item drops instead of after:
**does this item get rolled, or does Council award it?**

Everything not on this list keeps working exactly as it does today — RCLootCouncil,
everyone rolls, ML awards. This document only carves out the small set of items where
a lucky roll costs the raid real progression.

---

## Why we are doing this

Two items went to the wrong place recently. Both had the same cause, and it was **not**
the Loot Council.

1. **A near-BiS trinket went to an open roll.** Process gap — it should never have been
   in the roll pool.
2. **Deathbringer's Will was rolled. A survival hunter rolled 100 and won it.**
   The Fusion prio is `Warr/Feral > Hunt/Rog/Frost/Blood > Unholy/Enh`. Survival sits in
   the *second* band, behind every warrior and feral in the raid — and SV is the weaker
   hunter spec here, since the sheet names `Warr/Feral/MM` as the ARP-cap catalysts. The
   single best physical trinket in the instance went to roughly the fifth-best home for
   it, on one d100.

**Both items were rolled.** The dice do not read the prio sheet. Put a
progression-defining trinket in an open roll and it lands wherever it lands, and no
officer judgement afterwards can take it back.

> **A roll is not fairness — it is the absence of a decision.**

It *feels* fair because nobody chose. But "nobody chose" is exactly how the best item in
the raid ends up on a spec the reference sheet ranks fifth. Rolling is the right default
for the other two hundred items. It is the wrong tool for these thirty.

So: publish the tier list, then execute it.

---

## The three tiers

| Tier | Name | How it is awarded | Roll? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R** | **Reserved** | Council awards directly. No roll. | ❌ Never |
| **P** | **Prio group** | Council names the eligible group, those players roll among themselves | ✅ Within group |
| **O** | **Open** | Current system — everyone rolls, ML awards | ✅ Everyone |

**Default is O.** If an item is not written down in this document, it is Open. We are not
trying to council-control 232 items; we are ring-fencing about 30.

## How Council decides inside Tier R

When a Reserved item drops, the officer announcing it states these four things, in order.
Out loud, in raid chat, every time. It takes fifteen seconds and it is the entire point
of this document.

1. **Spec prio** — what the sheet says (quoted above).
2. **Actual upgrade size** — is this BiS, or a 40 dps sidegrade? A BiS-for-A beats a
   marginal-for-B even if B is the better player.
3. **Progression need** — does this item help kill the boss we are stuck on? LK tank
   and top-parsing DPS weigh heavier during prog.
4. **Attendance and performance** — the tiebreaker, not the opener. Uses our rankings.

**Ties go to attendance.** If two players are genuinely equal on 1–3, the one who has
shown up more gets it. That is the one place where "deserved" is measurable and not
arguable.

### The rule that prevents the last blow-up

> **A Reserved item is never put into a roll "to be fair."**

Not by the ML, not under pressure in the moment, not because the raid is impatient and
someone suggests just rolling it. Once the item is in the pool the decision is gone —
that is exactly how DBW ended up on a survival hunter. If an officer genuinely cannot
decide, the item is **held and decided after raid**, not diced.

Second, smaller rule: Reserved awards are not contested in raid chat. If the sheet says
Warr/Feral and it goes to a warrior, nobody relitigates it mid-pull on the grounds that
they also have a strength stat. Officer channel, after raid.

## Open questions for the officers

These are genuinely undecided and need a vote, not my opinion:

1. **How many Reserved items can one player receive per lockout?** Sheet warns *"don't
   load up one person early, it will take time for them to get the requisite items."*
   Proposal: soft cap of 2 Reserved items per player per lockout, overridable by
   unanimous officer call.
2. **Do we designate a permanent LK tank now?** Several Reserved items key off it
   (Flawless Fang, Juggernaut Band, most of Tank Loot). If we do not name one, those
   awards stall every week.
3. **Shadowmourne holder.** Separate decision, but it interacts with Glorenzelg —
   the sheet notes a warrior wants Glorenzelg *even if* they get Shadowmourne.
4. **Does this list apply to alts?** Recommend: Reserved items never go to alts while a
   main has the need.
